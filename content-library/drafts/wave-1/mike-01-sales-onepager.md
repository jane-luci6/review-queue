# What LUCI is (and is not)

LUCI is not the stack. The A/V stays. One interface to control, automate, and execute the entire guest experience.

- **Keep the A/V already on the floor.** Displays, speakers, lighting, sources, and control systems remain in place. LUCI works with and absorbs them. It does not replace them.
- **Run separate systems as one operation.** LUCI brings their controls into one interface, so the property does not have to treat every system boundary as an operating boundary.
- **Start with the property, not a catalog.** A first look maps the A/V you have, the interfaces your teams use, and the places where one change crosses multiple systems.
- **See your world in one place.** The walkthrough is built around the rooms, systems, and operating priorities already in front of you.

The next step is the conversation: your A/V, one operating picture, and the questions that matter on your floor.
