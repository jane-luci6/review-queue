# Ameristar Council Bluffs: a retrofit the floor never saw

For a property that cannot take its floor dark, an A/V retrofit presents a direct operational problem. The work has to move forward while the property keeps running. The transition cannot become a separate event that displaces the operation it is meant to support.

Ameristar Council Bluffs in Council Bluffs, Iowa, completed a full A/V retrofit in under three days. The floor never closed.

Those two facts belong together. The duration matters because the operation continued. This was not a project measured only by what was installed. It was a transition measured by whether the property could remain open while the work was completed.

The retrofit also moved A/V processing that had lived on the riverboat into the IT data center on land. That move established a clean boundary for what comes next. The boat can retire as a clean break, while the new buildout connects to the platform already running on land.

The property does not have to carry the old location of the processing into the next phase. It also does not have to start the A/V operation over when that phase arrives. The platform is in place for the transition.

Taken together, the retrofit and the move inland form one operational decision: keep the floor running now while establishing where control will live next.

The installation window served both the present operation and the transition ahead.

The Facilities Manager’s response, published in the case study, is concise: “Well done, LUCI team! This was the best installation — so seamless.”

For A/V, IT, and operations leaders considering a retrofit, Ameristar raises the questions that matter before a schedule is approved. Can the work happen around a live floor? Where should the processing live when the property changes? Will the next buildout connect to what is already running, or require another operating system beside it?

LUCI worked with the property’s A/V and brought its operation onto one platform. It did not replace the systems on the floor. The case is about continuity: through the retrofit, through the move from riverboat to land, and into the buildout ahead.
