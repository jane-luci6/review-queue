## Research (brief)

**AEO/GEO care more about extractable answers than hard CTAs.**
- Answer-first structure, self-contained paragraphs, named entities, FAQ/Q&A blocks, freshness dates help citation (AEORanks; WRITER GEO/AEO 2026; Frase entity GEO; Everyday on AI GEO writing).
- Princeton/IIT Delhi GEO paper (Aggarwal et al., KDD 2024): citations, quotations, statistics boost generative-engine visibility ~30–40%; keyword stuffing hurts. https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.09735
- Practitioner AEO guides still say close with a clear CTA — but that is engagement hygiene, not "pitch the whole platform."

**Early-funnel B2B still punishes self-focused product endings.**
- TOFU: ~80% educate / 20% product; hard demo CTAs on informational pages convert poorly vs soft next steps (Growth Lessons TOFU 2026; intent-mapping examples e.g. soft checklist vs hard trial).
- Best end CTAs feel like the natural next sentence of the argument, not a banner (First Page Sage B2B SEO).

**Implication for LUCI spotlights**
Do not end cold (leaves no path). Do not pivot into a platform brochure or walkthrough checklist (self-focused + mismatches early intent). Do: one definitional bridge sentence (entity-clear), two outcome messages the feature already proved, one low-friction invite (see it / demo), optional FAQ later for AEO — not required in v1 close.
