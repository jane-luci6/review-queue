# Wave 1 — Spotlight blog: revert preset

**Topic:** Try a look. Get the last one back.
**Persona:** Property A/V and ops who will not experiment because undo is a phone call.
**Source:** The Signal, Issue 02 (July 2026), “Set a revert preset.” Recast as a current-LUCI feature spotlight, not a customer how-to.
**CTA:** Book a walkthrough. See a look tried and put back.

**Cover:**
- The problem: a live floor punishes curiosity. If the new look is wrong, someone has to rebuild the last one.
- The feature: a revert preset in current LUCI.
- The benefit: they can try the next look because the last one is still there.
- What they can do: save the known state, try the change, revert if it is not the room they wanted.
- Optional teaser that shows the move (never “easy”): apply, don’t like it, revert.
- Close on the walkthrough, not a training manual.

Constraints for every piece
- Readers are new prospects (property A/V, IT, ops leadership). Not a customer operating manual.
- Always write A/V.
- LUCI works with and absorbs the systems already on the floor. It does not replace them. Do not name partners. Never call LUCI a layer.
- New LUCI is beta, property by property. Not a public splash. Do not write “we launched.”
- Do not invent ROI, percentages, or extra stats. Only facts in this brief.
- Never write the word “easy.” If a teaser shows the move, show the move.
- Named properties only if this brief names them. Do not add others.
- Institutional voice. Short. LUCI is the means, not the hero speech.
