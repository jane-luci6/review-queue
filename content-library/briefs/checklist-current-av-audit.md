# Wave 2 — Checklist: what can you control / view in your current A/V setup?

**Topic:** A diagnostic of the stack they already have. Not a LUCI procedure.
**Persona:** Property A/V, IT, and ops who cannot honestly say what they can see and change from one seat.
**Source:** Recast from Signal’s “one interface” argument (Issues 01–03 field stories and Mike 01). Do not teach clicks in LUCI.
**CTA:** Book a walkthrough. Bring this sheet. We’ll map it to one seat.

**Cover:**
- Title the sheet as a question, not a product: “What can you control and view today?”
- Boxes they can check: displays, sources, audio, lighting, announcements, signage, video walls — each: can I see status / can I change it / from where / who else can.
- Leave blanks for “which room / which console / which vendor.” Do not fill those with partner names.
- Ends on the gap: if the answers live on four screens, that is the demo. Not “set this in LUCI.”
- One close line: see the same questions answered from one seat.

Constraints for every piece
- Readers are new prospects (property A/V, IT, ops leadership). Not a customer LUCI how-to.
- Always write A/V.
- LUCI works with and absorbs the systems already on the floor. It does not replace them. Do not name partners or competitors (no Q-SYS, no brand fight). Never call LUCI a layer.
- New LUCI is beta, property by property. Not a public splash. Do not write “we launched.”
- Do not invent ROI, percentages, or extra stats. Only facts in this brief.
- Never write the word “easy.”
- Institutional voice. Short. LUCI is the means, not the hero speech.
- This is a lead magnet. It has to help before they have LUCI. Close with a walkthrough, not a LUCI procedure.
