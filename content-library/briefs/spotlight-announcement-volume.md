# Wave 1 — Spotlight blog: announcement volume

**Topic:** Not every announcement should be the same volume.
**Persona:** Property A/V, ops, and guest-experience leads who still blast every page at one level.
**Source:** The Signal, Issue 03 (August 2026), “Not every announcement should be the same volume.” Recast as a current-LUCI feature spotlight.
**CTA:** Book a walkthrough. Hear how announcements are scoped in LUCI.

**Cover:**
- The problem: one volume for bingo, emergency, and “the buffet is open” trains guests to ignore all of it — or hate all of it.
- The feature: announcement levels in current LUCI, by job, not one slider for the building.
- The benefit: the guest-facing A/V matches the message.
- What they can do: keep routine pages in their place; let the ones that must cut through, cut through.
- Optional teaser that shows the move (never “easy”): the announcement has a level, the way a zone has a look.
- Always A/V. No New LUCI dump.

Constraints for every piece
- Readers are new prospects (property A/V, IT, ops leadership). Not a customer operating manual.
- Always write A/V.
- LUCI works with and absorbs the systems already on the floor. It does not replace them. Do not name partners. Never call LUCI a layer.
- New LUCI is beta, property by property. Not a public splash. Do not write “we launched.”
- Do not invent ROI, percentages, or extra stats. Only facts in this brief.
- Never write the word “easy.” If a teaser shows the move, show the move.
- Named properties only if this brief names them. Do not add others.
- Institutional voice. Short. LUCI is the means, not the hero speech.
