# Wave 2 — Mike blog: the best things we build aren’t our ideas + pull-quotes

**Topic:** Platform and partnership as one offering.
**Persona:** Prospects who have been burned by a vendor that installs and disappears.
**Source:** The Signal, Issue 03, Minute with Mike: “The best things we build aren’t our ideas.” **New use, not a reprint.**
**CTA:** Book a walkthrough. Meet the team that stays.

**Cover:**
- The argument: the useful work comes from the property, not from our catalog.
- Platform + partnership. Control, automate, execute. A team that stays.
- Do not write that LUCI replaces other tech. Do not name partners.
- **Also deliver:** 4–6 short pull-quotes (paraphrased from the argument, clearly not invented first-person unless already published) that case-study blogs can use. No fake Mike quotations. If a line would need to be a quote, write it as a statement, not a quote.
- This is not a hire announcement and not Jane’s bio.

Constraints for every piece
- Readers are new prospects (property A/V, IT, ops leadership). Not a customer operating manual.
- Always write A/V.
- LUCI works with and absorbs the systems already on the floor. It does not replace them. Do not name partners. Never call LUCI a layer.
- New LUCI is beta, property by property. Not a public splash. Do not write “we launched.”
- Do not invent ROI, percentages, or extra stats. Only facts in this brief.
- Never write the word “easy.” If a teaser shows the move, show the move.
- Named properties only if this brief names them. Do not add others.
- Institutional voice. Short. LUCI is the means, not the hero speech.
