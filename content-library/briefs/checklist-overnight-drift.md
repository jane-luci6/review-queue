# Wave 2 — Checklist: what changed overnight?

**Topic:** Can they name what drifted, who could have done it, and whether last night’s look still exists.
**Persona:** Ops who open to a surprise floor.
**Source:** Signal 01 morning reset + 02 revert, recast as diagnosis. **If this becomes “set a revert preset in LUCI,” kill it.** It is a lead magnet about drift, not a how-to.
**CTA:** Book a walkthrough. See a look put back from one seat.

**Cover:**
- Morning questions: does the floor match last night’s close? What changed? Who can change it? Can we get the last look back without a rebuild?
- Yes/no. Blanks for “we don’t know.”
- LUCI only in the close: one interface, a known state, a way back. Not the steps.
- No partner names. Never write “easy.”

Constraints for every piece
- Readers are new prospects (property A/V, IT, ops leadership). Not a customer LUCI how-to.
- Always write A/V.
- LUCI works with and absorbs the systems already on the floor. It does not replace them. Do not name partners or competitors (no Q-SYS, no brand fight). Never call LUCI a layer.
- New LUCI is beta, property by property. Not a public splash. Do not write “we launched.”
- Do not invent ROI, percentages, or extra stats. Only facts in this brief.
- Never write the word “easy.”
- Institutional voice. Short. LUCI is the means, not the hero speech.
- This is a lead magnet. It has to help before they have LUCI. Close with a walkthrough, not a LUCI procedure.
