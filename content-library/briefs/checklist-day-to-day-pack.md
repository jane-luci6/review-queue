# Wave 2 — Checklist pack: day-to-day A/V (works without LUCI)

**Topic:** Three short sheets an operator can use this week even if they never buy LUCI. Then a light walkthrough close.
**Persona:** The person who actually opens the floor. Also the director who forwards it.
**Source:** Signal tips recast as property habits, not LUCI steps — Issue 01 morning reset, Issue 02 lighting, Issue 03 announcement volume. **Do not write “set this in LUCI.”**
**CTA:** After the sheets: if you want those three jobs on one interface, book a walkthrough.

**Cover — three sheets, one download:**
- **Opening the floor.** Does the room match the look you wanted at open? Sources, levels, walls, leftover looks from last night. A yes/no pass, not a LUCI reset how-to.
- **Lighting pass.** Is lighting in the same look as the rest of the room, or is it a second night of programming? Walk the zones. No console brands.
- **Announcement levels.** List the pages you run (routine, event, emergency). Are they the same volume? Which ones train guests to ignore you.
- Helpful first. LUCI is one closing paragraph, not the body.
- Never write “easy.” No partner names. No replace.

Constraints for every piece
- Readers are new prospects (property A/V, IT, ops leadership). Not a customer LUCI how-to.
- Always write A/V.
- LUCI works with and absorbs the systems already on the floor. It does not replace them. Do not name partners or competitors (no Q-SYS, no brand fight). Never call LUCI a layer.
- New LUCI is beta, property by property. Not a public splash. Do not write “we launched.”
- Do not invent ROI, percentages, or extra stats. Only facts in this brief.
- Never write the word “easy.”
- Institutional voice. Short. LUCI is the means, not the hero speech.
- This is a lead magnet. It has to help before they have LUCI. Close with a walkthrough, not a LUCI procedure.
