# Wave 1 — Sales one-pager: what LUCI is (and is not)

**Topic:** Same argument as the Mike 01 blog, for the room with Mark or Mike — not a website how-to.
**Persona:** A prospect sitting in a demo or a follow-up. Mark and Mike are in the room. Marketing does not talk over them.
**Source:** The Signal, Issue 01, Minute with Mike: “What you may not know about LUCI.” Recast as a one-page leave-behind.
**CTA:** Stay in this conversation. Next step is the walkthrough they are already in — not a support article, not a kiosk checklist.

**Cover:**
- One page. Headline, four short points, one close. No magazine layout.
- LUCI is not the stack. The A/V stays. One interface to control, automate, and execute.
- Works with and absorbs. Does not replace. Do not name partners. Never call LUCI a layer.
- What they can do in a first look: see their world in one place.
- Space for Mark or Mike to talk. This page does not close the deal.
- Not a customer Field Activation Guide. Not a booth how-to.

Constraints for every piece
- Readers are new prospects (property A/V, IT, ops leadership). Not a customer operating manual.
- Always write A/V.
- LUCI works with and absorbs the systems already on the floor. It does not replace them. Do not name partners. Never call LUCI a layer.
- New LUCI is beta, property by property. Not a public splash. Do not write “we launched.”
- Do not invent ROI, percentages, or extra stats. Only facts in this brief.
- Never write the word “easy.” If a teaser shows the move, show the move.
- Named properties only if this brief names them. Do not add others.
- Institutional voice. Short. LUCI is the means, not the hero speech.
