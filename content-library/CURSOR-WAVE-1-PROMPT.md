## What each draft is

- **Spotlights:** a short blog post. One current-LUCI capability. Problem, feature, benefit, what they can do, optional teaser that *shows the move*, demo CTA. Not a procedure. Not a customer how-to.
- **Case-study blogs:** a short door to the live case-study URL in the brief. Do not rewrite the case-study page. Only facts already public on that page. CTA: read the case study, then book a walkthrough.
- **Mike 01 blog:** a prospect argument (what LUCI is and is not). Not a reprint of the Signal column. Not a how-to.
- **Mike 01 sales one-pager:** headline, four short points, one close. One page. For the room with Mark or Mike. Does not close the deal. Not the Field Activation Guide.

## Voice and rules (every file)

- Readers are new prospects: property A/V, IT, ops leadership. Not current customers learning LUCI.
- Always write **A/V**.
- LUCI is the means, not the hero speech. Lead with their accumulated problem.
- LUCI works with and absorbs the systems already on the floor. It does not replace them. Do not name partners or competitors. Never call LUCI a layer.
- New LUCI is beta, property by property. Not a public splash. Do not write “we launched.”
- Do not invent ROI, percentages, extra stats, or superlatives. Only facts in the brief.
- Never write the word **easy**. If a teaser shows the move, show the move.
- Named properties only if the brief names them. Do not add Aliante. Do not add EverPass or Sunday Ticket.
- Institutional voice. Second person is fine. Short.
- Web copy, not Signal letter chrome. No “Welcome to The Signal.”
- No agent notes, kickers, or process language in the draft. The file is Jane-facing copy plus a title.
