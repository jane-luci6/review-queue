### Cycle: Selected pre-launch preview
- **WARM-PREVIEW-01** (`WARM/WARM-PREVIEW-01-private-preview-invitation.md`) #1 Private preview invitation
  - Timing: About 2–3 weeks before the preview webinar; past-demo and serious past-request prospects only
  - Purpose: Re-engage people who already invested meaningful time in LUCI by offering an early look at what changed. Do not state a release date.
  - CTA: Register for the private preview.
- **WARM-PREVIEW-02** (`WARM/WARM-PREVIEW-02-what-to-expect.md`) #2 What to expect
  - Timing: 7–10 days before the webinar
  - Purpose: Show three concrete changes they will see. Registrants receive agenda content; nonregistrants receive another registration opportunity.
  - CTA: Review the agenda or register.
- **WARM-PREVIEW-03** (`WARM/WARM-PREVIEW-03-registered-attendee-reminder.md`) #3 Registered-attendee reminder
  - Timing: 2 days before the webinar; registrants only
  - Purpose: Convert registrations into attendance without sending another product pitch.
  - CTA: Add to calendar or join the session.
- **WARM-PREVIEW-04** (`WARM/WARM-PREVIEW-04-personal-preview-follow-up.md`) #4 Personal preview follow-up
  - Timing: Next business day after the webinar; sent by Mark
  - Purpose: Acknowledge their prior interest, ask what stood out, and offer a direct conversation before the broader launch follow-up begins.
  - CTA: Reply to Mark or choose a time to reconnect.
- **WARM-PREVIEW-05** (`WARM/WARM-PREVIEW-05-recording-for-no-shows.md`) #5 Recording for no-shows
  - Timing: 2–3 business days after the webinar; registrants who did not attend
  - Purpose: Preserve the value of the invitation without asking for another live commitment. This can come from Mark with a brief personal note.
  - CTA: Watch the private-preview recording.
