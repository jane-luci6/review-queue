A new foundation for a growing property

When LUCI partnered with San Manuel Casino in 2018, the property operated six separate audio systems and four video systems, each managed independently. This patchwork of equipment and controls made even routine operations unnecessarily complex, with no single way to manage the property as a whole.

LUCI’s platform integrated the property’s existing endpoints and systems, unifying them over the property’s network under one control interface. Suddenly, what had been a collection of disconnected systems became a coordinated environment that was easier to manage and ready to grow. The project also helped drive LUCI’s move away from restrictive proprietary control systems toward the open, software-based orchestration approach it uses today.

When COVID temporarily closed the casino in 2020, San Manuel and LUCI used the opportunity to overhaul its audio infrastructure. LUCI designed the new system, procured equipment, and coordinated installation partners—expanding its role in engineering and project management ahead of the property’s major resort expansion.

Engineering alongside Yaamava’s expansion

As San Manuel advanced its major resort expansion, LUCI worked alongside the property’s design and construction teams, architects, and other technical disciplines to ensure its A/V systems were engineered to work seamlessly from the start. The multi-year development added expanded gaming space, a 17-story hotel tower, spa, pool deck, restaurants, bars, retail, and meeting spaces, transforming San Manuel Casino into Yaamava’ Resort & Casino.

LUCI contributed design, technical planning, construction coordination, and implementation throughout the project. The existing LUCI architecture scaled to accommodate new amplifiers, televisions, and control points without requiring separate audio, video, or control systems for each new space. The property continued to grow while maintaining a coordinated multimedia environment across the resort.

The next major development brought LUCI’s expertise to The 909, a world-class sports bar and food hall. Working with the architect of record and project teams, LUCI helped create an extensive LED environment featuring a 50-foot-wide Game Day display, an LED archway, and more than 150 screens throughout the venue.

Rather than introduce another standalone A/V system, LUCI expanded the backbone already in place at Yaamava’. The 909 joined the same multimedia management environment serving the rest of the property, extending centralized control to an entirely new guest experience.
