# LUCI campaign plan
One plan. Three streams. Follow this.

**The offer**
Platform + partnership. Control / Automate / Execute (Oversee sits under Control). New LUCI is beta, property by property — not a public launch. We sit above the endpoints. We do not replace Q-SYS. Never name Hub, CoreX, the tunnel vendor, or correlation IDs. Feature list is not final until Mike signs it.

Stay off live Mark and Mike conversations. Mark owns personal cadence and cold outbound. Mike owns demos, the feature list, and customization.

---

## 1. New LUCI Launch
Existing customers only.

**What.** A property-by-property beta. Not a splash. The newsletter teaser already ran. No more teasers. No case study. No sales deck in this GTM. No dedicated website release page — audit the pages we have.

Lead with: venue panels, staging, audit trails + in-product support, customizable interface.
Mention: audio groups, live map, add any endpoint, Entra/PIN sign-in, video-wall sync, display catalog.

Build, in this order:
1. What’s New one-pager (primary by benefit, short secondary list, why it matters to a property already on LUCI, CTA to plan the upgrade)
2. Upgrade guide / FAQ (who, when, prereqs, downtime, training, support — marketing writes it; CEO and field own the process)
3. IT technical companion (same features, more depth; private tunnel in language we can use, no vendor name)
4. Customer announcement email
5. LinkedIn announcement + one or two screenshot posts
6. Internal talking points
7. Signal follow-up as beta framing only

**Who.** Current LUCI properties. Ops and on-site IT. Not net-new buyers.

**When.** Mike signs the list. Then What’s New. Then the rest, one property at a time, over the next few months. Do not call this an August launch.

## 2. Demand gen / new leads
People who do not know us.

**What.** Same LUCI story we already tell: orchestration engine, one interface. Channels, in order: LinkedIn, the site (SEO + demo CTA), then outbound. Events last. Do not use the launch as the public splash.

Phase 1 is the warm-lead stream below.
Phase 2 is cold: LinkedIn and the site first, then Mark’s outbound.

**Who.** Casino / property A/V and IT who are not in a live deal with Mark or Mike.

**When.** LinkedIn after Mike returns the copy pass. Website messaging audit, then fall. A real demand-gen engine (paid, partners) is a later effort, not this plan.

## 3. Warm leads
People who already demo’d or asked for a demo.

**What.** Two short ActiveCampaign tracks. Do not write over live sales.

1. No response after a form or raise-of-hand: wait about 10 quiet days, then four emails.
2. Post-demo with no next step: wait about 30 quiet days, then four emails.

Any reply, booking, or active stage stops the track. Opens are not a trigger. Reuse Yaamava, Ameristar, Tachi, Sam’s Town, the Field Activation Guide, and The Signal. Form 7 is already on the site. Backfill old demo contacts from the last 12 months. Message: what’s new since you last saw LUCI.

**Who.** Warm prospects who are not in an active sales conversation. Mark’s book stays with Mark. Marketing only picks up the quiet ones.

**When.** Jane says go, then we build. Not live.

## One decision
The Net New doc said 3–5 emails over 2–4 weeks. The pipeline said two 4-email tracks with a 10-day and 30-day quiet. This plan uses the pipeline, because it keeps us off Mark and Mike. If you want the shorter Net New cadence instead, say so once.
