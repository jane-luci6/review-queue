Dependencies & critical dates

  • Beta live (Sep 30): Everything downstream depends on this. 30–45 days of testing and fixing before the product is ready for other customers.
  • Rollout decisions by Dec 1: Mike and Ops must determine eligibility, timing, sequencing, and customer actions. These feed the What’s New one-pager and the customer FAQ — Marketing cannot finalize either without them.
  • ActiveCampaign upgrade by Nov 3: Salesforce migration, pipeline stages, and workflow practice must be complete before any pre-launch emails are sent.
  • One-pager + website (Dec 4): First public-facing communication. Kicks off the pre-launch email sequence. Cloudflare setup with Nick is the prerequisite for the website.
  • Holiday closure (Dec 22 – Jan 2): LUCI is closed. Pre-launch emails pause naturally — last email Dec 18, first email back Jan 5.
  • Demo webinar (Jan 15): Customers and select warm prospects. Feature highlight reel produced from the recording.
  • Upgrade launch (Jan 29): Customer launch announcement goes out. Upgrade method (priority list or self-schedule) is TBD pending Mike’s decision.
