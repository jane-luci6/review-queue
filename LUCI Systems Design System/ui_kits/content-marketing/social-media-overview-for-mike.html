Platform

LinkedIn only — the LUCI company page (not your personal page, for now).

Cadence

2–3 posts per week — typically Tue / Wed / Thu. Jane sends you one page with the week’s drafts; you approve once before anything goes live.

Rules Jane follows

  1. No client names on in-progress photos or video.
  2. Completed-project posts follow the same rule — no client name unless you’ve approved naming that property.
  3. No client name mentions anywhere without your approval first.
