# LUCI social media production — handoff prompt for Claude

You're taking over LUCI's LinkedIn social media production. This brief gives you the goals, the brand voice, the hard rules, the weekly series to build, the post types, and the content sources. Where I leave the workflow open, **propose the best way to build it in your environment** — optimized for you to surface highly relevant, engaging ideas, generate the images/videos, and make it easy for me to review, select, and produce on a rolling 2–3 week calendar.

## What LUCI is

LUCI is an orchestration platform for casino and hospitality properties. One interface runs every A/V endpoint on the property — signage, guest TVs, the sportsbook, the pool, the ballroom, the casino floor — on one map. The product thesis is **variable reduction**: LUCI doesn't add a layer to a property's stack; it removes the variables (hardware, interfaces, vendors, remotes) the team has to manage. "One interface runs what a hundred used to."

## The goal

Run LUCI's LinkedIn presence — **company page only** (Phase 1; the principal's personal page is a later phase). **2–3 posts per week, Tue / Wed / Thu.** Consistency beats volume; if the bench is empty, post twice — never manufacture a third.

**Audience:** IT and operations leadership at casinos / gaming / hospitality — Dir, VP, C-suite. **Not** A/V technicians. Write for the person running the property, not the person racking the gear.

**The core message is the abstraction — "one interface to run everything."** LED walls, racks, before/after, and process video are the **hook, never the headline.** Every caption closes on the abstraction: one interface, one map, one team, one accountable system. Diagrams and conceptual visuals keep the focus on the primary message even when we show secondary benefits.

**No approval gate.** I post directly — Mike (our principal) has greenlit hitting the gas. You produce; I review and post.

## Hard rules (non-negotiable)

- **No progress / on-site field photos. Ever.** Casinos don't want their build shown before their grand reveal, and a worksite shot can invite an OSHA callout that isn't ours to defend. Even anonymized, don't do it. (A completed, post-go-live reveal is a *case study*, handled under case-study rules.)
- **Named case study only with the client's OK.** Unnamed is the default.
- **No timelines, scope, pricing, or go-live claims** unless the client approved them for public use.
- **No % boasts or unverified metrics.**
- **No competitor promotion. No pre-announcement partner news.** When in doubt, don't post.
- **Always A/V** — never "AV" or "A-V" — in body, captions, alt text, hashtags, and on visuals.
- **Never "layer" as a noun for LUCI.** Use *orchestration engine* / *LUCI orchestrates…*.
- **Describe what the platform does, not how it feels.** "Operate every endpoint from one interface" passes; "absurdly simple" does not.
- **Institutions, not adjectives.** Declarative, subtraction framing.
- **First-person (we/our) is the house voice** for case-study and thought-leadership posts — LUCI speaking directly.

## Four pillars (every post routes to one)

01 Visibility / control · 02 Align teams · 03 Activate guest experience · 04 A/V that scales & improves. No pillar, no post.

## The weekly series — Control the Whole Property (locked)

**Slot:** Tuesday. **Length:** 8–10 week season, then a two-week pause. **Medium:** a **30-second video** per week.

**Format:** The video opens on a property floorplan/map, highlights the **zone of the week**, pulls up that area, steady pan across it, then 2–3 specific shots of what LUCI controls there, with text callouts. Closes on "one interface / one map." **All visuals AI-generated** (map, zone footage, detail shots) — no stock footage, no client B-roll, no real property photography. **I generate the videos in Claude; you write the per-episode generation prompt and the caption.**

**Season 1 order:** Ep01 = **series intro** (shows the whole property, all zones highlight in sequence — introduces the series; does not single out one zone) → then casino floor → sportsbook → pool deck & cabanas → bingo hall → event & banquet → F&B → hotel lobby → conference rooms → marketing signage vs guest TVs → full-property map finale.

**Caption closes on one interface / one map — the zone is the hook, not the headline.**

## Post types

Every post is one of:
1. **Weekly series** — Control the Whole Property (Tue)
2. **Case study / snippet** — a published story or a one-line snippet + link
3. **Diagram-led** — the abstraction shown directly (I can provide canonical diagram images: What LUCI is, System architecture, Consolidation ledger, Embedded Operation orbit)
4. **Newsletter-derived** — reuse from The Signal (pull-quotes, articles, diagrams as standalone posts)
5. **Thought leadership** — Mike's framing / mined insights (first person)
6. **Industry / partner news** — lowest priority; commentary with a LUCI point of view, never a bare re-share

## Content sources (I'll hand you the raw material)

1. **Call / meeting transcripts** — Mike on record, recurring themes, the variable-reduction moments. Primary fuel for thought leadership + case-study angles. Ask me for the .docx transcripts; I'll provide them.
2. **Recent case studies** — full stories and snippets. Named only with client OK; unnamed is the default.
3. **The Signal newsletter** — reuse articles, pull-quotes, and diagrams as standalone posts.

**A standout from the transcripts to anchor the first thought-leadership post** — Mike on IPTV:

> "Anything that's a video source, we can turn it into a channel and send it to any TV in the property… reduce the number of signage players you have to pay for. Most of our clients have a hundred TVs with a dedicated player behind each, but you're only playing ten ads. So we put everything on the same IPTV LUCI system."

That's a ready-made "hundred-player problem" post — concrete, closes on one interface, and a diagram carries it without any field photo.

## The workflow — propose the best way to build it

I want it to be **super easy** for you to:
- **Farm highly relevant, engaging ideas** from the sources above — surface a bench of candidates, each with the angle, the post type, the visual, and a draft caption in the LUCI voice.
- **Generate images** (you do this) and **videos** (I do this in Claude, using your per-episode prompt).
- **Let me review, select, and produce** — I approve the ideas I want; you produce them (image + locked caption); I post.
- **Keep a rolling calendar 2–3 weeks out** across the Tue / Wed / Thu slots.

Propose the structure that works best in your environment — Artifacts, a canvas, a doc, a board, whatever lets me review and approve at a glance and lets you track status. **Don't assume I have a dev environment or a local server**; I review in whatever surface you produce. Optimize for: relevant ideas, easy image/video generation, and a fast review → select → produce loop for me.
