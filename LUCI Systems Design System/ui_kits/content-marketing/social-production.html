The hundred-player problem

Transcript · Mike, recurringproposed
The variable reduction principle
Thought leadership (Mike) · Thu · statement card or 3-slide carousel
VisualA bold type card — “100 interfaces → 1” in Space Grotesk, mint accent, on navy. Or a carousel subtracting interfaces one panel at a time. Agent generates.
AngleLUCI’s whole thesis in one line: subtract, don’t add. Every other platform asks your team to operate more; LUCI removes variables.
Caption
LUCI doesn’t add a layer to your stack. It removes the variables. More interfaces, more vendors, more remotes is the old model. LUCI’s job is the opposite — reduce the number of things your team has to manage. Hardware, interfaces, phone trees. Subtract. One interface runs what a hundred used to.

Per-post production

The fields for one post. The agent fills this when producing a picked idea; Jane reviews and posts.

Template — one post

Slot
Tue / Wed / Thu · date
Type
weekly series / case study / diagram-led / newsletter-derived / thought leadership / news
Caption
Written in the LUCI voice. Closes on the abstraction — one interface, one map, one team. First person (we/our) for case study & thought leadership.
Media
Image: agent generates, or pull a canonical diagram. Video: Jane generates in Claude (per-episode prompt). No field photos.
Hashtags
#AV #Integration #Hospitality …
Status
idea → picked → mocked → media ready → scheduled → posted

Hard rules (always)

  • No progress / on-site field photos — grand-reveal leakage + OSHA risk. Retired entirely.
  • Named case study only with client’s OK. Unnamed is the default.
  • Always A/V — never “AV” or “A-V.” Never “layer” as a noun for LUCI.
  • Caption closes on the abstraction — one interface, one map, one team. The gear is the hook, not the headline.
  • No competitor promotion; no pre-announcement partner news. When in doubt, don’t post.
