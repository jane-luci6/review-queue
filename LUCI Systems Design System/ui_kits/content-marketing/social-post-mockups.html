01 · Control the Whole Property

Tuesdays · weekly series · 8–10 week season · Ep01 = series intro, then one zone per week

Caption

Your property isn’t one room. It’s a casino floor, a sportsbook, a pool deck, a bingo hall, a ballroom, the restaurants, the lobby, the conference wing, and the marquee out front — each on its own remote, its own vendor, its own phone tree. Every Tuesday, we’re walking the property one area at a time — showing what it looks like when every area runs through one interface. Control the whole property. Not just the casino floor.

Media

30s video — series intro (Ep01). Property map opens, then nine zones highlight in sequence (casino floor → sportsbook → pool deck → bingo → events → restaurants → lobby → conference → signage), pull back to full map, end on “Control the whole property.” Full generation prompt: social-intake/control-the-whole-property/ep01-series-intro-claude-prompt.md

Notes
  • Ep01 introduces the series — shows the whole property, not one zone. Zone episodes start Ep02 (Casino floor).
  • Same map visual every Tuesday after; only the highlighted zone changes.
  • Caption closes on one interface, not on the gear.
  • Hashtags: #CasinoOperations #Hospitality #AV

02 · Case study snippet

Wednesdays · example: Ameristar Council Bluffs

Caption

Three days to a future-ready property. That’s the headline from our Ameristar Council Bluffs case study — and the story behind it is how a property modernizes A/V control without the rip-and-replace everyone fears. Every endpoint on one map. One team. One accountable system. Full case study in the link.

Media

Case study hero image or a canonical diagram — pull from ui_kits/case-studies/ameristar-council-bluffs.html (print/hero asset) or a relevant diagram from assets/diagrams/. No field photos.

Notes
  • Named case study (Ameristar Council Bluffs) — already published, so naming is fine. For unpublished work, keep it unnamed (the default).
  • Snippet format: one hook line + the abstraction close + link to the full story. Don’t dump the whole case study.
  • Closes on one interface / one map / one team.
  • Hashtags: #CaseStudy #AV #Integration #Hospitality

03 · Completed project reveal

Thursdays (or whenever a project goes live) · example: Aliante LED wall reveal — pending the wall going live

Caption

The wall is live. A few weeks ago we showed you the panels going up at Aliante in Las Vegas. Here’s the finished view — the same curved LED wall, running content across 28 separately controllable zones, all on one interface. For the team at Aliante, this screen is now just another endpoint alongside hundreds of others on their LUCI platform — one map, one place to change what every zone is doing. From bare panels to a live wall, on one map.

Media

Field photo of the finished, lit wall — content live across the 28 zones, ideally a shot that shows the split (different content per zone). Capture once the wall is live; file under social-intake/<that-week>/raw/. Pairs with the in-progress post (#02).

Notes
  • Post-go-live case study — only after the client’s grand reveal is public and they’ve OK’d the naming. This is the safe version of a “field” post (progress/on-site pics are retired).
  • Same first-person voice (we/our); same 28-zone detail, now shown running content.
  • Closes on one interface / one map.
  • Hashtags: #LED #AV #Integration #LasVegas
