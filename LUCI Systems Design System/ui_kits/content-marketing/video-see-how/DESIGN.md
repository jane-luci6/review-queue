# LUCI — See How Outro

## Visual identity

- Canvas: near-black `#030607`
- Primary ink: off-white `#F5F8FA`
- Primary accent: LUCI mint `#68E3BE`
- Muted ink: blue-gray `#A6B0B6`

## Typography

- Display and logo support: Syncopate Bold
- Tagline and contact details: Space Grotesk Medium/Semibold
- All copy remains uppercase or compact enough to read at video scale.

## Motion character

- Restrained, technical, and precise.
- The opening title fades in directly from black; later cards arrive with a short vertical rise and focus pull.
- Scene changes use a dark cinematic zoom rather than bright flashes or decorative effects.
- No gradients, glows, stock-tech motifs, or repeated geometric patterns.

## Beats

1. `0.0–3.3s` — “See how we did it” fades in from black and holds.
2. `3.3–9.8s` — LUCI logo and tagline resolve; after a short hold, website, email, and phone appear horizontally beneath them.
