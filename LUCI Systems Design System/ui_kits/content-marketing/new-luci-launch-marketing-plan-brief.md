# New LUCI Launch Marketing Plan — deck brief

## Purpose

Give Mike and Mark one clear operating picture of the New LUCI launch without walking them through the detailed campaign documents.

The meeting is for alignment, not a long decision workshop:

- Show how the launch supports customers, re-engages warm prospects, and creates net-new demand.
- Show the communications and assets Jane is building.
- Show how Marketing connects to Sales without defining the full workflow in the room.
- Discuss directional signs of progress without artificial quotas or conversion forecasts.
- Secure the two dependencies Marketing needs now.

## Audience

- **Mike, CEO:** How Marketing will generate demand, get more from current leads, communicate the customer journey, and use company resources.
- **Mark, Sales Director:** How campaign responses reach Sales, when Marketing automation stops, what comes back to Marketing, and why a shared ActiveCampaign pipeline matters.

## Narrative guardrails

- Keep the agreed six-section spine. Assets and content formats are supporting details, not new sections.
- Present one public launch with three coordinated audience paths.
- Do not turn the deck into a feature presentation. Feature details remain linked supporting material and are pending Mike's confirmation.
- Do not turn the meeting into a CRM design workshop. Jane and Mark will define stages, ownership, automations, and migration details after ActiveCampaign is approved.
- Treat Mike's rollout planning as work already underway. Marketing needs a reliable information loop, not ownership of rollout.
- Measures are directional evidence of movement. Do not include lead quotas, expected conversion percentages, or monthly pipeline forecasts.
- Use “A/V,” never “AV.” Do not call LUCI a layer.

## Main deck — final copy

### Slide 01 — Cover

**Display:** NEW LUCI

**Title:** Launch Marketing Plan

**Deck:** One coordinated launch for customers, warm prospects, and the next market.

**Footer:** Working session · Mike + Mark + Jane

**Speaker note:** This is the operating picture for the launch: who we are communicating with, what we are building, how it creates and advances demand, and what Marketing needs to execute.

### Slide 02 — What this launch needs to accomplish

**Kicker:** The job

**Title:** One launch. Three different outcomes.

**Existing customers**

**Make the release clear and actionable.**

Understand what is changing, see the new LUCI, and know how to move toward an upgrade.

**Warm prospects**

**Give quiet prospects a reason to return.**

Use what has changed—and proof from running properties—to reopen conversations.

**Net-new prospects**

**Create qualified interest in the full LUCI offer.**

Lead with recognizable property problems, then connect them to the platform and embedded team.

**Bottom line:** The release creates the moment. Each audience receives a different path through it.

**Speaker note:** The launch is not one message sent to one list. Customers need clarity and an upgrade path. Warm prospects need a credible reason to reconsider. Net-new prospects need to understand the operational problem before they need release details.

---

### Slide 03 — One launch, three audience paths

**Kicker:** Communications

**Title:** Each audience gets the next useful step.

**Existing customers**

**Path:** Preview → understand → upgrade

**Communications and assets**

- The Signal preview and seminar invitation
- Private seminar and recording
- What's New one-pager
- Launch email
- Upgrade guide and FAQ
- IT technical companion

**Warm prospects**

**Path:** Reintroduce → prove → reconnect

**Communications and assets**

- Selected private-preview invitations
- “What changed” email series
- Edited seminar walkthrough
- Consolidation proof
- Direct invitation to reconnect
- Prospect Pulse if they remain quiet

**Net-new prospects**

**Path:** Recognize → understand → respond

**Communications and assets**

- Problem-led acquisition emails
- Website, blog, video, and proof content
- Recorded walkthrough
- Demo invitation and immediate response
- Prospect Pulse for longer-term nurture

**Footnote:** Feature content is preliminary until Mike confirms the promoted list. Rollout details will populate the customer one-pager and FAQ as Ops decisions are made.

**Speaker note:** The customer communications have specific jobs beyond the newsletter: drive preview attendance, announce availability, and move properties toward an upgrade. Warm and net-new prospects do not receive the same launch sequence.

---

### Slide 04 — How Marketing will generate demand

**Kicker:** Demand generation

**Title:** Build discovery now. Build authority over time.

**Website**

**Purpose:** The source of truth and primary conversion surface.

**Build:** Updated positioning and capability content, New LUCI content, proof, and clear paths to a conversation.

**Blog**

**Purpose:** Earn search visibility and become a trusted source of operational intelligence for casino A/V, IT, and operations leaders.

**Build:** Useful answers to the problems properties encounter—not a stream of product announcements.

**YouTube**

**Purpose:** Make LUCI's workflows visible and create durable source material for search and answer engines.

**Build:** Seminar cutdowns, workflow demonstrations, and focused explainers, each supported by accurate transcripts and crawlable website summaries.

**Distribution and reinforcement**

- **LinkedIn:** Launch distribution, ongoing ideas and proof, plus a targeted paid test.
- **Email + The Signal:** Direct campaign communication and continued presence.
- **AVIXA + industry directories:** Consistent entity information, referral discovery, SEO, and AEO support.
- **Outbound support:** Content and sequences that reinforce Mark's existing prospecting.
- **Longer term:** Partner development and an event program in 2027.

**Speaker note:** This is not a list of disconnected tactics. Website, blog, and video create a growing source of useful information. LinkedIn, email, directories, and outbound distribute it and return people to the website. Video alone does not guarantee LLM visibility; transcripts, metadata, and corresponding crawlable pages make the material more usable.

---

### Slide 05 — How Marketing connects with Sales

**Kicker:** Sales connection

**Title:** Marketing creates momentum. Sales owns the conversation.

**Primary flow**

Campaign or content → reply, booking, or demo request → immediate confirmation → Mark or Mike follows personally → active Sales conversation

**If the conversation stalls**

Final personal attempt → explicit handback to Marketing → short recovery cycle → Prospect Pulse if the prospect remains quiet

**Operating rules**

- A reply, booked meeting, or active opportunity stops marketing automation.
- Opens and ordinary clicks inform reporting; they do not trigger a Sales handoff.
- Anyone already in an active Mark or Mike conversation stays out of automated nurture.

**Callout:** Jane + Mark will define stages, ownership windows, and handback details after the ActiveCampaign go-ahead.

**Speaker note:** Marketing is not replacing Mark's cadence. It is creating entry points, responding immediately, staying out of active conversations, and taking appropriate follow-up back when Sales explicitly returns it.

---

### Slide 06 — What progress will look like

**Kicker:** Early indicators

**Title:** Look for movement—not false precision.

**Customers**

- Customers engage with the preview and release information.
- Upgrade interest turns into concrete next steps.
- Recurring questions reveal where communication needs to improve.

**Prospects and Sales**

- Quiet prospects return to conversation.
- New inquiries and demo requests appear.
- Meetings happen and opportunities move forward.
- Recovery and nurture keep viable prospects from disappearing.

**Market visibility**

- LUCI appears across a broader set of relevant searches and answer surfaces.
- Useful website, blog, and video content begins accumulating reach.
- Directory and referral sources become visible.
- We can see which channels are creating conversations and pipeline.

**Speaker note:** We are not setting arbitrary lead quotas or forecast conversion rates for a new system. We will establish a baseline, watch for sustained movement, and learn which channels and messages produce meaningful conversations.

---

### Slide 07 — What Marketing needs now

**Kicker:** Immediate dependencies

**Title:** Two inputs unlock the work.

**01 · ActiveCampaign Pipelines**

Approve the ActiveCampaign upgrade so Marketing and Mark can work in the same operating system.

**Why now:** Salesforce records must be migrated, stages and automations configured, and the workflow practiced far enough ahead of launch that the system is ready before leads begin flowing.

**After approval:** Jane and Mark will work through the pipeline design and migration details separately.

**02 · Rollout information**

Keep Marketing current as Mike and Ops determine eligibility, timing, customer actions, support, and sequencing.

**Why now:** Those decisions supply the upgrade section of the What's New one-pager and the customer FAQ. Marketing does not own rollout; it owns communicating it accurately.

**Speaker note:** Most creative and product inputs are already available. These are the two dependencies that affect whether the customer journey and Sales handoff work as designed.

## Visual direction probes

Build only slides 01, 03, and 04 for each probe. The two options must use the same approved copy and differ in information architecture, not merely color or decoration.

### Direction A — Executive operating map

**Character:** Restrained, direct, and highly scannable. The deck feels like a concise operating plan.

- Dark cover with one display moment: “NEW LUCI.”
- Slide 03 uses three clean horizontal lanes. Each lane moves left to right from audience to path to assets. Use hairlines and whitespace rather than boxed cards.
- Slide 04 uses a structured system: website at the center, blog and YouTube as authority builders, and LinkedIn/email/directories/outbound as distribution routes. Keep the flow architectural and explicit.
- Favor flat fields, labels, short copy, and clear ownership.
- Best if the deck should feel operational and decisive.

### Direction B — Campaign ecosystem

**Character:** More visual and connective while remaining executive-level.

- Dark cover with a subtle campaign-field motif and one display moment: “NEW LUCI.”
- Slide 03 places the launch at the left or center and sends three distinct audience paths outward. Communications appear as milestones along each path.
- Slide 04 treats the website as the home base of a content ecosystem. Blog and YouTube build authority; distribution channels return audiences to the website and Sales path.
- Use scale and directional movement to communicate reinforcement across channels. Avoid decorative orbit lines that do not carry meaning.
- Best if the deck should make the size and interconnectedness of Jane's work immediately visible.
