## Caption — copy everything between the lines

---
One property. Four different logins.

The bartender's PIN opens the sportsbar screens and nothing else. The floor supervisor gets the casino floor. The VIP host gets the high limit room. The general manager sees every zone. Same system, different view for every role.

Access maps to responsibility, by design.
