How this space works

This is not a deliverables library — finished assets still live in Marketing Content, Messaging & brand, and the website repo. This is the input side: a place to drop raw material and let it accumulate until there’s enough to mine. Drop transcripts, written strategies, screenshots, deck exports, or notes into sources/ and ask for a review. The output is a growing backlog of concrete content ideas, tagged by channel and traced back to the source they came from.

The loop:

1You drop a source into sources/ (image, transcript, doc, link) and say “review this.”
2I read it, log it in the source log, and surface strategic themes + channel ideas.
3I flag any terminology drift against LUCI’s canonical voice before anything gets drafted.
4When an idea is ready, it graduates to the right deliverable space (website, newsletter, sales) and the status here moves to Live.

Strategic themes surfaced

Mined from the two filed Q-SYS integration strategy documents. These are the repeatable angles — every idea in the backlog ladders up to one of them.

  • Continuity of Control. LUCI’s value is preserving the A/V workflows buyers already rely on — not reinventing them. Same capabilities, fewer steps, cleaner interface.
  • Orchestration engine, not layer. Q-SYS handles native zone logic and routing; LUCI is the consistent control surface on top. (Source says “orchestration layer” — LUCI voice forbids “layer”; reframe as engine / LUCI orchestrates. See voice flags.)
  • Three target environments. Conference rooms (localized switching), hospitality / cabanas (guest-controlled zones), and divisible event spaces (combine / split as walls move).
  • The Honesty Protocol. Explicitly credit Q-SYS for backend routing. It defuses the “capability loss” objection from technical A/V teams and builds trust.
  • No capability loss. The buyer-fear counter-position: LUCI orchestrates an existing Q-SYS investment, it does not rip and replace it.

Voice & terminology flags

The source notes drift from LUCI’s canonical voice. Apply these corrections whenever an idea from the backlog gets drafted. Source phrasing on the left, what LUCI actually says on the right.

Source says LUCI says Why
“Lucy” “LUCI” Brand is LUCI. Confirm with Jane whether “Lucy” in the source is an alias, a codename, or a transcription artifact before publishing anything derived from these notes.
“orchestration layer” “orchestration engine” / “LUCI orchestrates…” LUCI voice forbids layer as a noun for LUCI. Use engine for positioning and “LUCI orchestrates” for headlines and capability copy.
“AV” “A/V” Always write A/V — in body, heads, captions, alt text, and diagrams. Never “AV” or “A-V.”
“native function” / implied ownership of zone logic Credit Q-SYS for backend routing Q-SYS is a named third-party product (QSC). Never imply LUCI owns native zone logic or routing — the Honesty Protocol is a hard guardrail.
“capability loss” as a fear “No capability loss” as reassurance Keep the phrase buyer-facing only as a reassurance, and always pair it with crediting Q-SYS so it reads as honest, not defensive.
