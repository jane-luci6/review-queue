# Casinos & Gaming — image-generation prompts

Prompts for the 11 image slots on the `/industries/casinos-gaming` page: one hero poster, five challenge cards, five solution cards. Paste each prompt into ChatGPT's image generator. Every prompt follows `luci-image-prompt-rules.mdc` — custom project photography, not AI stock.

**Reference consulted (real LUCI client photography):** `OneDrive/.../Project Media/Rio Rack Room` (rack room with LUCI tech), `Sams Town LED` (sportsbook before/after + LED video wall install), `Tachi LEDs` (casino floor install with scissor lift), `Osage LED Wall` (LED wall install under construction), `Aliante LED`. Casino is the **operations** side here — rack rooms, sportsbooks, video walls, odds boards, signage tickers, floor management — never gambling glamour.

**Aspect ratios:** hero = 16:9 (video poster frame; the page stage is 21:9 and crops with `object-fit: cover`). Challenge/solution cards = 3:2 (matches the 1280×853 the page already uses; cards cover-crop to fill).

## Notes

- Every prompt names a real body/lens, a focal length, a depth of field, and practical motivated lighting — that's what anchors "real photograph" over "AI render."
- People are candid, mid-task, never posed; faces are avoided with over-the-shoulder/back-of-head where possible to dodge generation artifacts.
- The "Avoid:" line is the negative prompt; in ChatGPT it's woven into the request, in Midjourney you can move it to `--no`.
- Casino is shot as operations infrastructure (rack rooms, sportsbooks, video walls, counters, tickers) — never gambling glamour.
- After generating, if a result still reads AI, diagnose which tell survived (plastic skin, perfect symmetry, blue haze) and re-prompt against that specific tell.
