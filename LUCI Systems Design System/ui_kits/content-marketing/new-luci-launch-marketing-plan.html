08LUCI

Sales connection

The handoff to Sales

Primary flow
Campaign or content → Reply, booking, or demo request → Immediate confirmation + Calendly link → Mark or Mike follows personally → Active Sales conversation
If the conversation stalls
Sales works it through a final attempt → Explicit handback to Marketing → Short recovery cycle → Prospect Pulse if they stay quiet
Operating rules
A reply, booked meeting, or active opportunity stops marketing automation. Opens and ordinary clicks inform reporting — they do not trigger a Sales handoff. Anyone already in an active Mark or Mike conversation stays out of automated nurture.

Jane and Mark will define stages, ownership windows, and handback details after the ActiveCampaign go-ahead — not in this meeting.
