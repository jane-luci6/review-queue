The job

One launch. Three different audiences.

Existing customers Make the release clear and actionable. Understand what is changing, see the new LUCI, and know how to move toward an upgrade.
Warm prospects Give quiet prospects a reason to return. Use what has changed — and proof from running properties — to reopen conversations.
Net-new prospects Create qualified interest in the full LUCI offer. Lead with recognizable property problems, then connect them to the platform and embedded team.

Progress indicators

We’ll know it’s working by the movement.

Customers

What we watch

Upgrade demand expressed, seminar attendance, and upgrade volume as the rollout proceeds.

Prospects

What we watch

Warm prospects re-engaging, inbound demo requests, and outbound calls that land a second conversation.

Visibility

What we watch

Website traffic on New LUCI pages, blog posts ranking for property problems, and LUCI cited by answer engines.

No conversion-rate targets or lead quotas. We watch for direction and momentum, then adjust.
