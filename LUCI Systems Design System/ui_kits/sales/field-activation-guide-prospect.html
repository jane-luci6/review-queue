LUCI Systems · Field Activation Guide 02

One floor. One platform. One strategy.

Before LUCI, most casino and resort teams work the floor separately. Marketing runs the signage, Operations shapes the gaming environment, A/V keep the screens and sound running — each in its own lane, on its own equipment, with few ways to coordinate.

Now every screen, zone, and audio channel can move on a single platform through LUCI. Marketing, Gaming and Operations, A/V and Facilities, and IT don’t have to coordinate separate equipment that happens to share a building. Everyone works from the same interface, through the same platform, toward the same guest.

That’s the real opportunity. When your teams decide together how the floor should work — which messages run where, how energy builds through the day, what happens when a jackpot hits or an event lets out — they’re building one intentional guest-experience strategy. Set the plan together, and each team can move fast in its own lane, because everyone knows how the pieces fit. Real-time changes still happen; they just happen against a shared plan.

That’s how this guide is built. The pages ahead are organized by team — Marketing, Gaming & Operations, A/V & Facilities, IT, Finance, and General Management. Each section gathers the ways LUCI changes how that team works: four use cases, each with a note on what it replaces. Turn to your team’s section, or read straight through to see how the whole floor fits together.

Plan the floor together. Then activate it.

LUCI Systems · Field Activation Guide 03

Marketing & Guest Experience

Every screen on the property is now yours to program by zone, by daypart, in real time.

The reach that used to live on a handful of dedicated signs now covers the whole floor.

  • 01

    Push a message to every screen in under two minutes.

    With LUCI, you can push a marketing message from your own device to the whole floor or a single set of zones, and it’s live in moments. Then it drops back to the scheduled plan when you’re done.

    • React while the moment still matters, instead of after it passes
    • No ticket, no call, no waiting on another team’s queue
    • Every screen stays current — none stranded on yesterday’s message

    Todaya content swap means a ticket to the signage vendor and a wait.On LUCImarketing pushes it themselves, in minutes.

  • 02

    Daypart your restaurant promotions to catch guests at the decision point.

    Schedule a dining spot to run across the floor in the hour before lunch and again before dinner — the windows when guests are deciding whether to stay on property or head out — then reset the regular rotation automatically. Set the timing once and it repeats every day.

    • Capture covers that would otherwise walk out the door
    • Reach guests anywhere on the floor, not just the screens by the restaurants
    • Lift on-property spend in the same move

    Todaydining promos run on a fixed loop, timed to no one’s appetite.On LUCIthey hit the floor in the hour before each meal, every day, on their own.

  • 03

    Put your video wall to work for marketing: run it full or split it into zones.

    A video wall is the focal point of a space — the sportsbook, the bar, the entrance. With LUCI, set it up as a set of layouts (presets): one runs the full wall as one big display; another carves out side panels for promo ads, odds, or branding. Switch between them with a tap, or schedule the switch for the moment it matters.

    • One wall, multiple layouts — switch with a tap or on a schedule
    • Carve out zones for marketing without giving up the main feed
    • Reconfigure the layout yourself — no integrator visit

    Todaya video wall lives in one layout, and a change means an integrator visit.On LUCIrun it full or split it into zones, and switch with a tap or on a schedule.

  • 04

    Build an event campaign that escalates automatically.

    Schedule a rotation that grows an event’s presence — more zones, more frequency — as the date approaches, and fills your slow dayparts with loyalty and dining offers in between. Build it once; it ramps and resets on its own.

    • Build momentum toward an event without touching it daily
    • Put historically slow windows to work instead of waiting them out
    • Return the floor to normal automatically, with no stale content left running

    Todayan event push means daily manual changes leading up to it.On LUCIone countdown schedule ramps and resets on its own.

The bigger picture

The floor is now your largest marketing channel. Run it like one.

Gaming & Operations

The floor’s environment is now a single instrument you can play — the energy guests feel is something you set on purpose, by zone and by hour.

A floor that responds to the time of day, to a jackpot, to the room you actually have keeps players engaged and the energy where you want it.

  • 01

    Set your floor’s audio to match the room’s energy, all day.

    Build an audio schedule that moves the way your floor does — lighter in the morning, building through the afternoon, peaking in the evening, and easing back after the late-night crowd thins. Each zone follows its own curve, and the transitions happen on their own.

    • Keep the room feeling alive without a manager riding the faders
    • Match energy to the crowd you actually have at each hour
    • Give every shift the same intended sound

    Todaya manager rides the faders by feel, shift to shift.On LUCIeach zone follows its own curve all day, on its own.

  • 02

    Make every jackpot a floor event, automatically.

    When a jackpot hits, a coordinated preset fires in that zone — accent lights pulse, audio swells for a beat, nearby screens flash a celebration — then everything resets on its own. The response is calibrated per area so it fits the space. If the jackpot preset doesn’t already exist, the guide below walks you through building one from scratch.

    • Turn one guest’s win into energy the whole floor feels
    • Create a sense of action that keeps nearby players engaged
    • Deliver it every time, without any manual effort

    Todaya jackpot wins and the floor barely notices.On LUCIlights, audio, and nearby screens fire a calibrated celebration, then reset.

    Page the property without cutting the music.

    LUCI’s announcement system uses priority ducking — a page overrides a zone’s audio for a moment, then the regular audio returns on its own. Page a guest, call a team member, or run a promo over the floor audio from any device, without a walk to the booth and without killing the music.

    • Page from anywhere — no walk to the booth, no dedicated mic
    • The music dips for the page, then comes back on its own
    • One system for guest pages, team pages, and promo spots

    Todaya page means a walk to the booth and the music cuts out.On LUCIpage from anywhere and the music dips for the moment, then returns on its own.

    Reconfigure the floor’s video wall for a tournament or event.

    The big video wall on the floor isn’t stuck in one layout. Set it up as a set of presets — full wall for the tournament finale, zoned for leaderboards and branding — and switch between them with a tap, or schedule the switch for tip-off. When it’s over, one tap returns it to the everyday layout.

    • One wall, multiple layouts — switch with a tap or on a schedule
    • Full wall for the moment that matters, zoned for the rest
    • Reconfigure it yourself — no integrator visit

    Todaya tournament means a manual wall reconfig or an integrator visit.On LUCIfull-wall or zoned presets switch with a tap or at tip-off, then revert when it’s over.

The bigger picture

The energy on your floor is no longer luck. It’s a setting.

A/V & Facilities

The whole property’s audio and visual environment runs from one interface — every display, source, and zone, on or off site. The walk to a rack or a manual reset is the exception now, not the routine.

Build it once, then manage by exception. The floor holds its baseline on its own, and your day shifts from execution to oversight.

  • 01

    Build the whole property’s schedule in one session, then let it run.

    Spend some time configuring audio, displays, and zone behavior for every daypart, weekday and weekend, from one place. Once it’s built it runs itself, with a locked baseline underneath so the floor stays consistent no matter who opened — and you update any piece in real time without taking the rest offline.

    • Trade daily execution for oversight and the occasional adjustment
    • Eliminate the drift that creeps in shift to shift
    • Change one thing without disturbing anything else

    Todaythe schedule is rebuilt shift by shift, drifting as it goes.On LUCIbuild it once and it holds a locked baseline, editable in real time.

  • 02

    Resolve service requests from wherever you are — including after hours.

    A manager flags that the bar audio is off; you open that zone from your phone, adjust the level, and confirm it’s right — on property or off. Physical presence is reserved for genuinely physical problems, and every change logs itself.

    • Close out most issues in minutes, without a walk or a drive
    • Know whether a call really needs an on-site trip before anyone makes it
    • Keep an automatic record without a separate documentation step

    Todaya bar-audio complaint means a walk to the rack, after hours or not.On LUCIopen the zone from your phone, adjust, confirm — and it’s logged.

  • 03

    Swap any source without a truck roll.

    When a channel changes, a feed needs updating, or you want to repurpose a screen, edit the source on the map yourself. Update it once and every display drawing from that source picks up the change — no integrator visit, no truck roll, no waiting on a vendor.

    • Change a channel or feed yourself, the same day you decide to
    • One edit propagates to every display using that source
    • Skip the integrator visit and the vendor wait

    Todaya source change means a ticket and a truck roll to the rack.On LUCIedit the source yourself and every display using it picks up the change.

    Configure a private event once, then activate it and let it revert on its own.

    Build the full A/V setup for a banquet, concert, or private event as a named preset — custom audio mix, branded screen content, zone-specific settings — and enter the number of minutes until it should revert. The preset runs for the event and returns the property to its baseline on its own, with no tap to switch it off.

    • Deliver a polished event without rebuilding it each time
    • Hand activation to anyone — it’s one tap, not a checklist
    • Guarantee the floor returns to normal the moment it’s over

    Todaya banquet means rebuilding the A/V setup each time and a tap to tear it down.On LUCIbuild it once as a preset; it runs and reverts on a timer.

The bigger picture

The best service call is the one you can close quickly because you can manage it from anywhere.

IT & Technology

A/V is now standard infrastructure on the network you already run — visible, governed, and logged like everything else. No proprietary black boxes, no specialist truck roll to find out what’s happening.

Visibility, access control, and an audit trail — the basics you expect from any managed system, now extended to every screen and speaker on the floor.

  • 01

    Add an endpoint to LUCI — video, audio, or lighting.

    When a new display, speaker zone, or lighting system lands on your network, bring it onto LUCI yourself — no integrator truck roll. Enter the device’s IP, name it, assign it to a venue, and it’s on the map for every authorized user on that venue to reach.

    • Stand up new hardware yourself, on your schedule
    • One click-path covers video, audio, and lighting
    • Every endpoint you add is visible, tagged, and managed like the rest of the network

    Todaya new endpoint means an integrator truck roll and a wait.On LUCIenter its IP, name it, assign a venue, and it’s on the map.

  • 02

    Keep every config, backup, and guide for your system in one place.

    Your system’s config files, backups, user guides, and walkthrough videos already live in one spot your team can reach any time — so a lot of issues you can clear yourself, without a phone call to your integrator or a ticket into a vendor queue.

    • Self-serve the common issues instead of waiting on a vendor
    • Every config and backup is documented and reachable
    • File issues through the support site when you do need help

    Todaya common issue means a phone call to the integrator or a vendor ticket.On LUCIself-serve the common issues from one spot your team can reach any time.

  • 03

    Define who can change what — by role, by zone, by team.

    Set role-based permissions centrally so Marketing controls content, A/V controls schedules and levels, and no one reaches outside their lane — enforced by the platform and managed from a single administrative view.

    • Give each team exactly the access it needs, nothing more
    • Reduce the risk of an accidental change to the wrong zone
    • Adjust permissions in one place as roles change

    Todayaccess is informal and easy to over-grant.On LUCIrole-based permissions keep each team in its lane, enforced centrally.

  • 04

    See device health across the property at a glance.

    Zoom out on the property map and LUCI clusters every endpoint into a single colored circle — green means the cluster is all online, yellow means at least one device is offline, red means at least one is powered off. Click a cluster to break it apart and see exactly which device needs attention, so a dead screen gets caught before a guest ever notices it.

    • Spot offline and powered-off devices before a guest does
    • Read the whole property’s health in one glance, not zone by zone
    • No polling or walk-arounds — the map tells you where to look

    Todaya dead screen is noticed by a guest first, or caught on a walk-around.On LUCIthe map clusters every device and flags the one that needs attention before a guest does.

The bigger picture

A/V stops being a vendor’s black box and becomes endpoints on your network — monitored, permissioned, and logged like every other system you run.

Finance

A/V moves from a capital line to a predictable platform investment — and, for the first time, becomes something you can measure. The cost structure your old A/V budget was built around no longer applies.

Replan the capital, recover the contract spend, and start measuring the return.

    Replan the capital you’d reserved for the next A/V refresh.

    The proprietary hardware that needed a full refresh every five to seven years is replaced by a software platform on a predictable annual line. Capability now arrives through updates rather than capital purchases, which changes the refresh request that used to drive your A/V budget.

    • Free the capital you’d set aside for the next hardware cycle
    • Trade a lumpy refresh expense for a predictable annual line
    • Forecast A/V spend instead of bracing for the next big ask

    Todaya proprietary hardware refresh looms every five to seven years.On LUCIcapability arrives through updates on a predictable annual line, freeing the capital.

  • 02

    Cut or renegotiate the vendor and integration contracts LUCI replaces.

    A large share of A/V’s old operating cost lived outside the property, in vendor and integration contracts that existed because the expertise didn’t. That control is internal now, so the contracts written around the old limitations are yours to consolidate or end.

    • Recover spend on scopes your team now handles directly
    • Consolidate overlapping vendors into one managed relationship
    • Retire contracts built around limitations that no longer exist

    TodayA/V cost sits in vendor and integration contracts built around expertise you didn’t have.On LUCIthat control is internal, so the contracts are yours to consolidate or end.
