Shape the interface around your property and operation.

- **Customizable interface** — Bring your property’s identity into LUCI.

## 6. Layout instructions for GLM

### Layout decision: Split Proof Ledger

Keep Flow C’s announcement → promises → close structure, but remove its repeated promise index. Build the white middle section as three stacked, full-width ledger rows:

- Use **“The 3 Promises of New LUCI”** once as the middle-section title.
- In each ledger row, reserve roughly **30% on the left** for the promise number, header, and short lead.
- Use the remaining **70% on the right** for that promise’s features.
- Inside the feature area, set each feature on one compact horizontal line: **feature name at left, 5–8 word teaser at right**. Stack the lines with hairline separators; do not turn them into cards or equal columns.
- Let row height follow content. The third promise may be slightly taller because it has four features, but all three headers retain equal visual weight.
- Keep the announcement, headline, single subhead, and date together in the top field. The headline is the dominant read; the date remains visible at a glance.
- Make the bottom a compact information close: the more-information line, URL placeholder, then contact details. Do not style it as a download CTA.

### Remove from Flow C

- Remove **“The promise — three controls”** and its three-item index band.
- Remove the complete upgrade-path / fulfillment block: no confirm-plan step, laptop step, activation sequence, arrows, or path label.
- Do not add a download CTA.
- Keep **Add any endpoint** and **Display model catalog** off the sheet.
- Do not add proof metrics or client names.
