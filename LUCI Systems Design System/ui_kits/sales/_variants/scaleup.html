Top New Features

01

Greater control of the room

Venue panels

Scoped control on a venue tablet. It shows only the allowed controls for that room.

Staging

Prepare screens, sources, and volumes while the current look runs. Apply on cue, then save as a preset.

Audio group control

Adjust linked audio zones together without losing their tuned balance, or change one zone on its own.

02

Flexible control of your view

Customizable interface

Apply approved photography, marks, colors, and light or dark themes so LUCI reflects the property’s identity.

Live map flexibility

Shape the live floor plan around the property’s actual spaces, with device status kept current on the map.

Live screen view

See the content playing on any screen from an iPad, formatted as it appears live.

03

Deeper control over security

Audit trails

Trace each action to a person, preset, or schedule and export the record when needed.

Live monitoring

See device, display, and audio status live, including when a device stops responding and returns.

Sign-in & session control

Use email, PIN, or Microsoft credentials, then end sessions or message everyone currently in LUCI.

In-product support

Raise a request from a device, incident, or error with the relevant context and logs already attached.
