A new version of LUCI is coming

Putting the power of programming in your hands

The new version of LUCI puts more control in your hands—with better support behind it.

Release date January 19

The 3 Promises of New LUCI

Promise 01

Greater control of the room

Operate from the room with the controls you need.

  • Venue panels — put room controls where the work happens.
  • Staging — build the next look before applying it.
  • Audio group control — adjust linked zones without losing their balance.
Promise 02

More flexible control of your view

Shape the interface around your property and operation.

  • Customizable interface — bring your property’s identity into LUCI.
  • Live map flexibility — match live floor plans to physical spaces.
  • Video/LED wall layout sync — pull processor layouts in without rebuilding.
Promise 03

Deeper control over security

See activity, govern access, and bring support closer.

  • Audit trails — trace each action to a person and a time.
  • Live monitoring — see device, display, and audio status live.
  • Sign-in and session control — control who signs in and stays active.
  • In-product support — raise requests with context already attached.
