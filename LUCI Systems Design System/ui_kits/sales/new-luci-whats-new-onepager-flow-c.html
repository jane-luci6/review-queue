What’s new, in detail

01

Greater control of the room

Venue panels

A tablet in the space puts control with the person running it, from a ballroom events manager to a guest at a cabana. Assign it to a venue and it adopts that room’s endpoints, showing only the controls an administrator allows.

Staging

Choose screens, sources, and volumes for the next look while the current one keeps running. Nothing in the room changes until Apply, and any staged set can be saved as a preset to run again.

Audio group control

Move several zones together so their tuned balance holds, set every zone to one level, or adjust a single zone on its own. Linked rooms stay in proportion instead of being reset one by one.

02

Flexible control of your view

Customizable interface

Sign-in and splash screens carry the property’s photography and marks, in light or dark, with themes drawn from an approved set. The interface reads as the property’s, not a generic console.

Live map flexibility

The floor plan stays tied to the devices on it through a live connection, so what is on screen matches the room. Shape the plan to the property’s actual spaces rather than a generic layout.

Live screen view

Open a display on an iPad and see the content playing there, laid out the way it is on the wall. Confirm what guests are seeing without walking to the screen.

03

Deeper control over security

Audit trails

Every action is recorded against a person and a time, including whether a person, a preset, or a schedule triggered it. Search by device or user, and download the record when you need it.

Live monitoring

Device, display, and audio status stay current. When a device stops responding, an incident opens and closes when it returns, so a fault has a measured duration.

Sign-in & session control

People sign in by email, PIN, or the Microsoft credentials they already use. Administrators can end a session and post a message to everyone currently in LUCI.

In-product support

Raise a request from the device, incident, or error itself. It arrives with what happened, what changed, and the logs already attached, and a person decides what gets sent.
