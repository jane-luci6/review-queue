Services

Scope of work.

Property-wide A/V network, audio modernization, IPTV/video distribution, and convention upgrade integrations across the Sportsbook, Ballrooms, and Studio B.

1. LUCI Network Infrastructure

Deploy a dedicated A/V network to support all LUCI-managed systems, isolated from the corporate network and designed for future scalability. Core network equipment includes one Cisco C1300-20XS core switch, ten Cisco C1300-48MGP-4X 48-port switches, three Cisco C1300-8MGP-2X 8-port switches, thirty 10Gb single-mode LR SFP modules, and two 1Gb multi-mode SX SFP modules. User interfaces comprise six Apple iPads in Bouncepad enclosures — one rack-mounted iPad in the Sportsbook equipment rack, the remainder at designated operator locations.

2. Landside Retail & Lobby

Phase 1 audio modernization reuses existing ceiling speakers while upgrading processing, amplification, and zoning through LUCI and Q-SYS. Audio zones include the Casino, Landside Common Areas, Restaurant, Porte Cochere, Lobby Bar, Retail Areas, Ballrooms (with room combine capabilities), Studio B, and the New Elevator Lobby (new speakers installed by others). Work replaces existing amplification with new Q-SYS amplifiers, upgrades all casino audio zones, and provides infrastructure to replace the currently non-functional microphone system. Studio B (convention hall / multipurpose room) receives AVPro Edge encoders and projector decoders with a 10-inch touchscreen control interface, designed to accommodate a future remodel into convention and poker operations.

3. Ballroom Systems

Ballroom 1 and Ballroom 2 (each four divisible rooms) receive new A/V distribution systems replacing legacy HDBaseT infrastructure. Each ballroom includes a new HDBaseT encoder/decoder system, new projectors (owner-installed), 137-inch manual projection screens (owner-installed), reuse of existing XLR infrastructure for room combination and presentation audio, single-mode fiber extension between SR8 and the Ballroom A/V IDF (owner-installed), and reuse of existing audio speakers and cabling.

4. Sportsbook & Sports Bar

Deploy a centralized IPTV and A/V control solution supporting sportsbook operations. Includes COM3000 installation (scalable to all TVs in the low rise), LG STB-6500 integration for the existing Novastar video wall, and local source encoders (10 channels). Display support covers 32 Sportsbook displays, 47 Sports Bar displays, and 22 booth displays. Audio routing for soundbar systems uses ML2x2 interfaces, with a dedicated single STB-6500 for the Sports Bar video wall.

5. Central A/V & Head-End Infrastructure

SR1 (main IT data center) is repurposed from the existing FanDuel rack as the LUCI head-end. Equipment includes redundant DSP processing, the LUCI application server, a dedicated firewall, the core network switch, a 48-port access switch, OneLINK players, local source encoders, ML4 interfaces, and rack accessories with fiber infrastructure. Audio sources previously distributed throughout the property are centralized within SR1 where practical.

6. Remote Equipment Rooms

SR21 supports second-floor casino audio — remove legacy Biamp processing, relocate sources to SR1, install one 48-port switch and two amplifiers. SR11 is reserved for future OneLINK players and encoder expansion. The Hidden Amplifier Room supports one amplifier for the Lobby and Porte Cochere with verification of existing orange data cable termination. SR10 covers the Registration audio zone with one 48-port switch and two amplifiers. SR3 receives one amplifier and one 48-port switch. SR14 integrates paging interfaces (Intellix and Viking) with one amplifier and one 8-port switch. SR2 provides fiber interconnect to SR1/SR3, one 48-port switch, and one amplifier.

7. Specialty Areas

Insomnia Stage promotions integrate paging and background music into the local mixer (owner to verify copper connectivity to SR2). Pool receives an audio-only deployment — one amplifier and one 8-port switch — with existing coax television infrastructure remaining.

8. Out of Scope

The 32nd Floor Restaurant is excluded from this phase — existing Russound audio system, existing COX television distribution, and standalone infrastructure with no direct connectivity to SR1. The Spa is also excluded — existing Russound audio retained across approximately 40 treatment rooms, with no network connectivity modifications. These areas are excluded unless specifically added by change order.

Assumptions

This scope assumes all third-party hardware, including fiber optic network infrastructure and required connectivity for IPTV and audio distribution, is provided and properly configured by others. Projectors and projection screens in the Ballrooms are owner-installed, as is the single-mode fiber extension between SR8 and the Ballroom A/V IDF. Any on-site installation labor, hardware mounting, or third-party programming not explicitly stated is excluded. Action items to confirm before deployment: floorplan with markup of ballroom projection, audio, and control panels; Studio B multi-mode fiber path to SR1; sportsbook ODDS on IPTV or behind dedicated TVs; dedicated ISP cable modem; OneLINK room relocation and encoding; Hidden Amp electrical room orange data cable termination; and Insomnia Promotions data cable run to the assigned SR room.
