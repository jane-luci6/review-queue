Services

Scope of work.

Property-wide A/V network, audio modernization, IPTV/video distribution, and convention upgrade integrations across the Sportsbook, Ballrooms, and Studio B.

Project Overview

Provide a complete deployment of the LUCI platform across the property, including a dedicated A/V network, centralized control infrastructure, audio system modernization, IPTV/video distribution (beginning with the Sportsbook and Sportsbar), and convention upgrade integrations. Existing infrastructure will be reused wherever practical to minimize costs while providing a scalable foundation for future expansion.

1. LUCI Network Infrastructure

Deploy a dedicated A/V network to support all LUCI-managed systems, isolated from the corporate network and designed for future scalability.
