A new LUCI platform release is on the way.

Putting the power of programming in your hands

New LUCI gives your team more direct control to shape the experience, run the venue, and see what needs attention—without waiting for a programmer to make every change.

For LUCI customers and prospects.

01

Operate

Control now lives in the venue, not just the back office.

Put the right controls in the space, prepare the next change while the room keeps running, and bring support into the work itself.

Feature in focus

Venue panels

Control lives in the venue. A tablet in the space puts it in the hands of the person who runs it, with controls scoped to that space.

More proof

  • Staging Build the next look while the current one keeps running. Apply on cue.
  • In-product support Raise a support request from the device, incident, or error itself, with context and relevant logs already attached.
02

Make it yours

Your maps, your look — the system belongs to the property.

Bring the property’s identity and physical layout into the interface operators use every day.

Feature in focus

Customizable interface

Sign-in and splash screens carry the property’s own photography, marks, and colors, with curated light and dark themes.

More proof

  • Live map flexibility Load the property’s own floor plans and orient the map to match the physical space.
03

See and secure

What’s happening now, what happened, and who’s in the system.

Give the team a current operating view, a record of activity, and control over active access.

Feature in focus

Live monitoring

See the real-time state of every device, display, and audio zone—what is on, what is off, and what has a fault.

More proof

  • Audit trails Every action is recorded against a person and a time, including what triggered it.
  • Sign-in & session control Email, PIN, or Microsoft Entra sign-in, with administrator control over who is active.
Proof strip
