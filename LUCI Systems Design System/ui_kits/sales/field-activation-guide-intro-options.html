Before → Now, four ways.

Same copy, same order (paragraph one as normal text, paragraph two as a highlight/callout). Four treatments to compare for scannability without losing detail. Pick one and I’ll drop it into the hub.

Option 1 · Pull-quote callout

Paragraph two becomes a brand-canonical callout: 4px mint left bar, sharp corners, bold Space Grotesk. The eye reads the plain paragraph, then lands on the bold quote.

One floor. One platform. One strategy.

Teams worked the floor separately. Marketing ran the signage, Operations shaped the gaming environment, A/V kept the screens and sound running — each in its own lane, on its own equipment, with few ways to coordinate.

Every screen, zone, and audio channel moves on a single platform. Marketing, Gaming, A/V, and IT aren’t working separate equipment that happens to share a building. They’re working the same floor, through the same platform, toward the same guest.
