Technology Overview

The COB Advantage

COB embeds the LED chips into the substrate — eliminating SMD vulnerabilities. Paired with NovaStar for broadcast-quality visuals.

  • Superior image quality

    Seamless surface with no visible pixel gaps — smooth gradients and true blacks at any distance.

  • Enhanced durability

    No exposed LEDs means resistance to impact, moisture, and dust — built for high-traffic environments.

  • Energy efficient

    Up to 30% lower power consumption than equivalent SMD displays, reducing operational costs.

  • Wide viewing angle

    160° horizontal and vertical angles ensure consistent image quality from any position.

  • Easy maintenance

    Front-serviceable modules with magnetic mounting allow individual panel replacement in minutes.

  • Future-ready

    HDR10 support, 16-bit color depth, and 3840Hz refresh rate for broadcast-quality content.

NovaStar H-Series processing. Enterprise-grade video processing with 4K@60Hz input, seamless switching, HDR tone mapping, and remote management via NovaStar Cloud. Redundant power and signal paths target 99.99% uptime.

Terms & Warranty

LUCI Systems stands behind every installation with comprehensive warranty coverage and responsive service support. LUCI and DesignLED cover panels from edge to edge, pixel to pixel — including LED tiles, receiving cards, power supplies, and frame systems.

Warranty duration: 2-year comprehensive limited parts warranty, commencing from the date of product delivery. Duration is as per the project contract unless stated otherwise. This warranty applies only to the original purchaser and is not transferable.

What’s covered

  • All LED display panels and modules
  • Video processing equipment
  • Mounting hardware and structures
  • Cabling and connectors
  • Labor for warranty repairs

Warranty void if

  • Unauthorized modifications or repairs
  • Physical damage from misuse
  • Power surge without proper protection
  • Exposure beyond rated IP conditions
  • Failure to follow maintenance guidelines

Service process

  • 24/7 support hotline
  • Remote diagnostics via NovaStar Cloud
  • On-site response within 48 hours
  • Replacement parts shipped next-day
  • Quarterly preventive maintenance available

Not covered

  • Content creation or scheduling
  • Third-party equipment or software
  • Cosmetic wear from normal use
  • Acts of nature or force majeure
  • Consumables (filters, etc.)

Extended warranty. Additional coverage years are available at time of purchase. Contact your LUCI Systems representative for extended warranty pricing. This is a limited parts-only warranty; if a defect is discovered during the warranty period, DesignLED must receive prompt written notice within forty-eight (48) hours. Defective components are returned to DesignLED, and repaired or replaced units remain warranted for the balance of the original warranty period.
