# CUST-WEBINAR-01 — Seminar invitation

- **Audience:** Operations and IT at current LUCI properties
- **Cycle:** Pre-launch webinar
- **Timing:** About 2–3 weeks before the webinar
- **Purpose:** Give customers an early look at New LUCI. Include the What’s New asset so they can see the features and customer benefits before deciding to attend.
- **CTA:** Review what is coming and register for the live preview.
- **Status:** draft v1 — for Jane review

## Strategy

### What we’re writing and why
A customer invitation that gives the LUCI Upgrade Overview enough weight to help a cold reader decide whether the private preview is useful. The email introduces the control promise, establishes that LUCI support remains in place, and offers two sequential actions: scan the overview, then register.

### For whom
Operations should recognize more direct control over rooms and day-to-day changes. IT should recognize stronger visibility, access control, and support context. The copy keeps both groups in one shared customer conversation rather than splitting the message.

### Best way to reach them
Send from a named LUCI customer-facing lead through the marketing platform, with a human signature. Keep the body under 100 words, use short paragraphs, and send about 2–3 weeks before the preview. Put the overview link before registration so attendance is an informed choice.

### How to make impact while staying professional
Open on the practical change in authority, not an announcement flourish. Call the event a private customer preview, name a few concrete capabilities, and state the continuing LUCI partnership plainly.

### Primary message
Review how New LUCI will give your team more day-to-day programming control, then register for a private customer preview.

## Winner
**Direction 2 — See it before you decide**

It wins because the first email’s distinct job is to let customers understand the changes before making a time commitment. The final borrows Direction 1’s partnership language and Direction 3’s concrete capability sweep without turning the invitation into a feature list.

## Self-audit
- More client control and continuing LUCI support both appear.
- New LUCI is “on its way”; no release, launch, or availability date appears.
- No hardware language or claim that LUCI displaces existing technology; no partner/vendor name; no forbidden positioning or rollout framing.
- The webinar is a private customer preview, with no sales, pricing, or demo framing.
- Active voice, cold-reader context, “A/V” spelling rule observed, no hype or em-dash chain.
- Only approved placeholders appear; the exact webinar date appears only as `[WEBINAR DATE]`.
- Body is under 150 words; preheader is under 90 characters.
- “We’ve never lost a client” is intentionally omitted. It does not help the reader decide whether to review the product changes or attend, and forcing it here would turn a customer preview into a credibility pitch.
- Tension: this email necessarily has two linked actions because Jane’s brief asks customers to review the overview before registering. Their order makes the sequence clear.
