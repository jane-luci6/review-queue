- **Audience:** Cold net-new prospects completing the acquisition cycle without a reply, booking, or active opportunity
- **Cycle:** Acquisition
- **Timing:** 5 days after email 3
- **Purpose:** Connect the orchestration engine and embedded team, then invite qualified interest into a conversation.

- **Status:** draft v1 for Jane review

## Strategy

### What we’re writing and why
The acquisition close connects the platform and permanent team, then invites prospects with a real consolidation priority into a focused demo.

### For whom
Operations and IT leaders who now understand the problem, proof, and product view but need to know who stays accountable after implementation.

### Best way to reach them
Send five days after NET-ACQ-03 from the same sender. Keep one qualification cue in the close and use the booking URL as the only asset.

### How to make impact while staying professional
Lead with hardware-choice preservation, make the embedded team's continuing work concrete, and place the retention claim beside that accountability rather than using it as a headline.

### Primary message
LUCI combines an orchestration engine that preserves hardware choice with an embedded team that stays accountable, giving qualified prospects a reason to book a demo.
