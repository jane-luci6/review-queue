# NET-ACQ-04 — Platform, team, and invitation

- **Audience:** Cold net-new prospects completing the acquisition cycle without a reply, booking, or active opportunity
- **Cycle:** Acquisition
- **Timing:** 5 days after email 3
- **Purpose:** LUCI brings varied A/V into one interface and keeps a team monitoring, diagnosing, and supporting the environment.
- **CTA:** Book a focused demo.
- **Status:** draft r3 for Jane review

## Strategy

### What we're writing and why
Close the acquisition cycle by pairing the platform with the people who remain accountable, then offer a focused demo.

### For whom
Operations and IT leaders who understand the problem and product but still need confidence in the continuing relationship.

### Best way to reach them
Send five days after NET-ACQ-03 from the same sender. Use the booking URL as the only CTA.

### How to make impact while staying professional
Make the Monday-morning work of monitoring and diagnosis more concrete than a generic partnership promise.

### Primary message
LUCI brings varied A/V into one interface and keeps a team monitoring, diagnosing, and supporting the environment.

## Three directions

### Direction 1 — Monday morning
_The selected direction, revised for whole-campaign consistency._

Dear #Name#,

Different rooms can keep different hardware. Staff should not need different operating habits for each one.

LUCI standardizes the operating path across those rooms. If an endpoint fails, the LUCI team receives an alert and sees device context and logs before the support conversation begins.

Software can earn the meeting. Accountability has to stay after it.

That continuing work matters: we've never lost a client.

If fewer interfaces and handoffs are on your list, book a focused demo: [BOOKING URL]

### Direction 2 — Map and people
_Pair a shared property map with the people watching the environment._

Dear #Name#,

LUCI runs a property's A/V from one interface, giving staff one map for rooms, screens, and sources even when the equipment varies behind it.

The same view helps the LUCI team monitor the environment, diagnose issues, and support the people on site. A shared screen is useful. Shared accountability is the part that survives a busy Saturday.

If that combination fits an active priority, book a demo built around your property: [BOOKING URL]

### Direction 3 — Focused invitation
_Qualify the demo around a current room, screen, or workflow problem._

Dear #Name#,

A LUCI demo is most useful when there is a specific knot to untangle: a ballroom with too many control paths, a wall nobody can verify remotely, or staff moving between different interfaces.

LUCI brings the property's A/V into one operating interface. Its team continues to monitor, diagnose, and support the environment as needs change.

We will skip the generic tour and start with the knot.

Book a focused demo: [BOOKING URL]
