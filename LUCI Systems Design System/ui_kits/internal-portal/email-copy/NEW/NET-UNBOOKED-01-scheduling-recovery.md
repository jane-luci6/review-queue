# NET-UNBOOKED-01 — Scheduling recovery

- **Audience:** Net-new demo requesters explicitly handed back to Marketing after personal Sales attempts
- **Cycle:** Unbooked request recovery — after Sales handback
- **Timing:** Provisional: about 7 days after Mark or Mike's final attempt and explicit handback
- **Purpose:** Offer one clean path to schedule without restarting the acquisition pitch.
- **CTA:** Pick a time for the walkthrough.
- **Status:** draft v1 for Jane review

## Strategy

### What we’re writing and why
A scheduling recovery note for someone who asked to see LUCI but did not book after personal Sales outreach. Its only job is to make the next step easy without replaying the acquisition argument.

### For whom
Net-new prospects who made a real request, then went quiet. They may still be interested, or timing and format may have created friction. The copy leaves room for either explanation without implying they owe LUCI a response.

### Best way to reach them
Send from LUCI Marketing only after Mark or Mike explicitly hands the prospect back. Keep the note short, give one direct booking link, and route any reply to the sales owner who handled the request.

### How to make impact while staying professional
Name the unfinished scheduling step plainly. Do not summarize LUCI, list capabilities, or manufacture urgency. The useful gesture is a clean calendar path plus permission to explain a timing or format issue.

### Primary message
The walkthrough you requested is still available through one direct scheduling link, and a reply can resolve any timing or format issue.

## Three draft directions

### Direction 1 — One direct path
Dear #Name#,

You asked to see LUCI, but we never found a time that worked. Rather than restart the conversation, here is one direct path to the walkthrough.

Pick a time: [BOOKING URL]

If timing or format is what held things up, reply here and tell us what would work better. Your reply will go to the sales owner who handled your request. We’ll take it from there.

### Direction 2 — Name the friction
Dear #Name#,

Sometimes the meeting request is still right, but the calendar or format is not. If that is what happened with your LUCI walkthrough, you can choose a different time here:

Choose a time: [BOOKING URL]

If a standard walkthrough is not the right format, reply with what would be more useful. The sales owner who handled your request will receive your note directly.

### Direction 3 — Keep it simple
Dear #Name#,

We’ll keep this simple. The LUCI walkthrough you requested is still available, and this link is the quickest way to put it back on the calendar:

Schedule the walkthrough: [BOOKING URL]

If the timing has changed, reply here instead. Your note will go directly to the sales owner who handled your request, without asking you to repeat the reason you reached out.

## Winner
**Direction 1 — One direct path**

It wins because it acknowledges the request, identifies the incomplete step, and moves immediately to one scheduling action. The final borrows Direction 2’s permission to change the format and Direction 3’s assurance that the prospect will not have to begin again.
