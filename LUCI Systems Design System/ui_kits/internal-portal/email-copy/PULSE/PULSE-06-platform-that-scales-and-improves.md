# PULSE-06 — A platform that scales and improves

- **Audience:** Warm and net-new prospects eligible for long-term nurture
- **Cycle:** Every 4–6 weeks
- **Timing:** Month 6
- **Purpose:** Frame LUCI as an A/V platform refined continuously and built to extend when the property expands. Use New LUCI as proof that the investment becomes more capable over time without forcing the property to start over.
- **CTA:** See how the platform scales and improves.
- **Status:** draft v1 for Jane review

## Strategy

### What we’re writing and why
The final Pulse email elevates the decision from today’s feature set to the property’s next phase. It frames expansion and continuous refinement as one investment argument, with beta product capabilities as evidence.

### For whom
Property and technology leaders planning added spaces, endpoints, or operating demands who want to avoid parallel systems and repeated starting-over cycles.

### Best way to reach them
Send from LUCI Marketing in Month 6. Open on expansion, define how LUCI extends the existing environment, link to the LUCI Upgrade Guide, and leave a direct conversation path.

### How to make impact while staying professional
Show continuity of platform, team, and operating model. Use the exact retention line as relationship proof and keep all New LUCI language in beta.

### Primary message
A/V infrastructure should extend with the property and become more capable over time while the same operating model and team remain in place.
