# PULSE-06 — A platform that scales and improves

- **Audience:** Warm and net-new prospects eligible for long-term nurture
- **Cycle:** Every 4–6 weeks
- **Timing:** Month 6
- **Purpose:** Show how LUCI extends the same A/V operation into a new ballroom while the platform continues to gain practical control.
- **CTA:** See how the platform scales and improves.
- **Status:** draft r2 for Jane review

## Strategy

### What we’re writing and why
Close the six-month series on physical expansion and product continuity. Define LUCI and New LUCI for a reader who saw none of the earlier emails.

### For whom
Property and technology leaders adding rooms, endpoints, or venues who want to avoid a second control system.

### Best way to reach them
Send from LUCI Marketing in Month 6. Use one ballroom-expansion scene and make the LUCI Upgrade Guide the only CTA.

### How to make impact while staying professional
The extra-logins line keeps the argument human. The exact retention line supports the long-term platform and team claim.

### Primary message
LUCI extends the same A/V operation into a new ballroom, while New LUCI shows how the platform continues to gain practical control.
