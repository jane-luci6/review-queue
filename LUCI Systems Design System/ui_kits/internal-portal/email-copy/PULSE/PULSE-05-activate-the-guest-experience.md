# PULSE-05 — Activate the guest experience

- **Audience:** Warm and net-new prospects eligible for long-term nurture
- **Cycle:** Every 4–6 weeks
- **Timing:** Month 5
- **Purpose:** Show the gap between passive screens and purposeful, real-time guest moments. Tie LUCI’s ability to activate every endpoint to a tangible guest-experience outcome the prospect recognizes.
- **CTA:** See how LUCI turns passive screens into guest moments.
- **Status:** draft v1 for Jane review

## Strategy

### What we’re writing and why
The fifth Pulse email shifts from internal operation to the visible guest result. It uses a specific watch-party changeover to distinguish installed endpoints from an activated experience without claiming revenue or engagement gains.

### For whom
Property operations and marketing leaders who have substantial screen, wall, and audio investments but still depend on disconnected manual changes to create a live moment.

### Best way to reach them
Send from LUCI Marketing in Month 5. Use a short scene with staging and a venue panel, then link to a visual workflow proof.

### How to make impact while staying professional
Let the reader picture the current program staying on while the next look is prepared, then show one approved action coordinating screens, wall content, and audio.

### Primary message
Coordinated staging and approved room controls turn installed screens and audio into a purposeful guest moment.
