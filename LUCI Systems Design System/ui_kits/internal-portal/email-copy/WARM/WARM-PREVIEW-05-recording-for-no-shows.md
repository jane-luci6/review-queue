- **CTA:** Watch the private-preview recording.
- **Status:** draft r2 for Jane review

## Strategy

### What we’re writing and why
A brief service note that preserves the value of the invitation after a missed session.

### For whom
Registrants who did not attend and may still want the product view on their own time.

### Best way to reach them
Send from Mark 2–3 business days later. Put the recording first and the written guide in a quiet secondary line.

### How to make impact while staying professional
Remove guilt, define the product change for a cold reader, and let the recording do the work.

### Primary message
The recording gives them the same early product view without another live commitment.
