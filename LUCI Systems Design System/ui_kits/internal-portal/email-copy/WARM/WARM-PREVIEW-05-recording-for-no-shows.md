# WARM-PREVIEW-05 — Recording for no-shows

- **Audience:** Registered selected warm prospects who did not attend
- **Cycle:** Selected pre-launch preview
- **Timing:** 2–3 business days after the webinar; registrants who did not attend
- **Purpose:** Preserve the value of the invitation without asking for another live commitment. This can come from Mark with a brief personal note.
- **CTA:** Watch the private-preview recording.
- **Status:** draft v1 for Jane review

## Strategy

### What we’re writing and why
A brief note from Mark that preserves the value of the selected invitation after a missed session. It gives the recipient the same early product view on demand and avoids asking them to make another live commitment.

### For whom
Selected warm prospects who registered but did not attend, are not in an active opportunity, and may still be interested despite a scheduling conflict. They have prior LUCI context, but the copy does not treat their absence as a signal of intent.

### Best way to reach them
Send from Mark 2–3 business days after the webinar. Acknowledge the missed session without guilt, put the recording first, and offer the LUCI Upgrade Guide only as a written companion.

### How to make impact while staying professional
Make access easy and judgment-free. The note should read like Mark is making sure they receive what they registered for, not using the missed webinar to create another meeting ask.

### Primary message
You can still get the selected private-preview view on your schedule, with no additional live commitment.

## Why the final won
**Direction 1 — Same early look, on demand**

It sounds most like a short note from Mark, removes any guilt around the missed session, and explicitly protects the recipient from another live commitment. The final borrows Direction 2’s beta context and Direction 3’s recognition of their prior LUCI history.
