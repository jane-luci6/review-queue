# WARM-PREVIEW-05 — Recording for no-shows

- **Audience:** Chosen registrants who missed the private preview and remain outside active sales conversations
- **Cycle:** Selected private preview
- **Timing:** 2–3 business days after the webinar; registrants who did not attend
- **Purpose:** The recording gives them the same early product view without another live commitment.
- **CTA:** Watch the private-preview recording.
- **Status:** draft r3 for Jane review

## Strategy

### What we're writing and why
A brief service note that preserves the value of the invitation after a missed session.

### For whom
Registrants who did not attend and may still want the product view on their own time.

### Best way to reach them
Send from Mark 2–3 business days later. Put the recording first and the written guide in a quiet secondary line.

### How to make impact while staying professional
Remove guilt, preserve the value of the invitation, and let the recording carry the product explanation without repeating email 01's LUCI definition.

### Primary message
The recording gives them the same early product view without another live commitment.
