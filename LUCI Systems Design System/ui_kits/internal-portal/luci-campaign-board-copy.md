**Title:** New LUCI Launch Campaign

**Objective:** Launch the new LUCI publicly, drive customer upgrades, re-engage warm leads, and generate net-new demand.

**Lede:** One coordinated launch with three audience paths: current customers see what is new and how to upgrade; warm leads get a reason to return; net-new prospects meet the full LUCI platform through the updated website and campaign.

**Channels**
- Website (updated): The LUCI story, new capabilities, proof, search, and a path to a demo. The primary surface for customers and for new leads arriving from any channel.
- LinkedIn (organic): Launch announcements and capability posts. Reaches customers and prospects already aware of LUCI; supports every audience path.
- Demo seminar / webinar: A planned launch event. Operators and IT walk the new LUCI. Date, platform, and speaker TBD.

## Existing customers
- Audience: Operations and IT at current LUCI properties.
- Objective: Move current properties to the new version, hardware-first, in a set priority order.
- Strategy: Every current customer receives a new laptop with the new LUCI and the security tunnel stack already loaded. Properties ship and upgrade in a priority order. The customer verifies on the new laptop, then officially switches. The laptop that is running the property stays untouched — no packet download onto that machine, no change-management on the live box.
- Message: The upgrade arrives as a new machine they can prove before they switch. More control where the work happens: venue-specific panels, changes prepared before they go live, a record of every action, support in context, and an interface matched to the property.
- Assets: What's New one-pager; Upgrade guide and FAQ (new laptop, verify, then switch; priority order); IT companion (how New LUCI sits above the property's existing A/V environment, and that the tunnel stack arrives already loaded); customer announcement email; internal talking points for the field; Signal newsletter release article.
- Primary: Venue panels (control in the space, scoped to the venue). Staging (build the next look while the current one keeps running). Audit trails and in-product support (every action recorded; start a support request from the device, incident, or error, with the relevant context already attached). Customizable interface (the visual reveal: property photography, marks, colors).
- Also in the release: Audio group control. A live map that reflects the floor. Add any endpoint from LUCI. Email, PIN, or Entra sign-in. Video wall layouts pulled from the processor. A central catalog of hospitality display models, pushed to the property.
- Measures: Upgrades scheduled and completed; feature adoption at 90 days; recurring support questions.
- Link: Preliminary feature list → #coming-soon/new-luci-feature-list

## Warm leads
- Audience: Prospects who requested or completed a demo, and are not in an active sales conversation.
- Objective: Re-engage prospects who have seen LUCI but gone quiet, and move them back into a sales conversation.
- Strategy: Use the launch to reopen quiet conversations with prospects who have already shown interest. Active opportunities remain in the sales cadence.
- Message: LUCI now puts more control in the venue, makes complex changes safer to prepare, and gives operations and IT a clearer record of what happened.
- Assets: Re-engagement email series (3–4 emails) after a form with no reply; same after a demo with no next step; ongoing nurture, likely The Signal; proof pull of consolidation numbers across running properties (displays, zones, walls, racks removed), not new-feature-specific; a reply or a meeting ends the track.
- Measures: Re-engaged conversations; demos re-booked; deals advanced.

## Demand gen
- Audience: Casino and property A/V and IT who are not already in a conversation with us.
- Objective: Generate qualified interest and demos from prospects who have not encountered LUCI.
- Strategy: Earn attention through search and targeted paid social, then convert it on the website. The updated site and a new blog carry the story and the SEO surface; paid LinkedIn puts it in front of the right titles at mid-size casinos; outbound supports the prospects Mark is already working.
- Message: Separate A/V systems create separate workflows, vendors, and points of failure. LUCI consolidates them into one operating environment, with a team that stays.
- Outbound channels and tactics: Blog, launching with the new website; SEO program; paid LinkedIn ads targeting c-suite, VP, and Director levels at mid-size casinos; outbound sequence supporting Mark's existing prospecting cadence; inbound demo response and nurture emails; demo seminar / webinar (date, platform, and speaker TBD).
- Measures: Inbound leads by source; qualified opportunities; demos booked; pipeline created.
