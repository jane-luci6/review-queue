1Private preview

  • WARM-PREVIEW-01You looked at LUCI before, so you get a private first look at the beta and its new daily controls.“A private look, because you looked before”
  • WARM-PREVIEW-02What the preview covers: three real workflows that put more control in staff hands, with LUCI still behind them.“Three workflows in the private preview”
  • WARM-PREVIEW-03Registrants only: date, calendar button and join link in one short note.“Your private preview details”
  • WARM-PREVIEW-04Mark asks for one honest reaction and offers a conversation only if it helps.“What looked useful this time?”
  • WARM-PREVIEW-05Missed it? The recording, on your own time, no second commitment.“Your private-preview recording”

2Introduce & engage

  • NET-ACQ-01Routine A/V changes turn into relay races across separate controls and vendors. Sound familiar?“How many steps does one display change take?”
  • WARM-REENGAGE-01Problem first: one wrong screen should not need several interfaces and an off-site expert.“When dinner inherits yesterday's brunch menu”
  • NET-ACQ-02One approved property count shows varied rooms and hardware running from one interface.“What consolidation looks like on property”
  • WARM-REENGAGE-02A short reel of the live map in motion; the full recording stays a quiet option.“Let the live map explain”
  • NET-ACQ-03A short reel: staff changing rooms, checking screens and reading activity history themselves.“Watch one access sequence in New LUCI”
  • WARM-REENGAGE-03Approved counts show scale, then the one-interface point, and the team that has never lost a client.“The denominator behind one interface”
  • NET-ACQ-04One interface plus a team that monitors and supports it. Bring your knottiest A/V problem to a demo.“What stays after the walkthrough”
  • WARM-REENGAGE-04Test the beta against one real room, wall or workflow in a focused conversation.“Want to test one real LUCI workflow?”

3Respond / demo

  • NET-DEMO-01Request received; pick a time, no extra pitch.“We received your LUCI demo request”
  • WARM-RESPONSE-01Mark has your reply or booking; you will not need to repeat yourself.“Mark has your response”
  • NET-DEMO-02Mike or Mark asks for one priority so the walkthrough starts with your operation.“I saw your LUCI request”
  • WARM-RESPONSE-02Mark picks up from your earlier LUCI context and asks which next step helps now.“I'll start with what matters now”

4Recover

  • NET-UNBOOKED-01Your walkthrough request is still open: one calendar link, nothing to repeat.“Did the calendar win?”
  • NET-POSTDEMO-01One workflow from your demo, worth a second look if it is still a priority.“Is this workflow still active?”
  • NET-UNBOOKED-02Active scheduling stops here; the link stays open for when timing improves.“No explanation required”
  • NET-POSTDEMO-02We close the follow-up but keep the context; a reply picks it back up.“Closing the follow-up here”

5Nurture: Prospect Pulse

  • PULSE-01A time-sensitive screen change, handled from one interface, with the team still behind you.“Why is one screen change a scavenger hunt?”
  • PULSE-02A refresh can consolidate control and keep the useful hardware in service.“Can a refresh keep useful hardware?”
  • PULSE-03One shared map so marketing, IT, operations and leadership see the same screen status.“What is actually playing in the lobby?”
  • PULSE-04Audit history plus LUCI monitoring keeps a visible A/V issue connected from report to fix.“The wall is dark and doors open in 20”
  • PULSE-05Stage a ballroom changeover quietly and run it in one coordinated cue.“The ballroom changes in ten minutes”
  • PULSE-06A new spa joins the same A/V operation, and the platform keeps gaining practical control.“Will the new spa need another control path?”
