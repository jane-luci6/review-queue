02 Primary platforms

We’re building both channels from a standing start — LinkedIn now, X in the next week or two once Nick secures access — ahead of prospecting in the next month or two. The goal right now is audience and message, not leads.

01 · Approach

Zero-click by design

  • Posts deliver their full value in the feed. No outbound links: LinkedIn cuts reach ~60% on posts carrying one, and the link-in-comments workaround is now penalized too.

  • Our live site is also still the old brand, so sending new-brand traffic there undercuts the post. Links switch back on when the new site launches.

  • X doesn’t penalize links the same way — but until the new site is live, links stay off both channels for consistency.

  • Judged on dwell, comments and follower growth—not clicks.

02 · Message sequencing

Establish one idea. Then widen the story.

Now

One interface, every technology on it. Orchestration, not replacement.

Next

Expand into visibility and control, team alignment, guest experience, and A/V that scales and improves.

Throughout

Build visually first so the pain and the fix are seen rather than described.

Real rack density, live floor plans, and consolidation diagrams and imagery. A reader should recognize their own property before we mention the product.

03 · Content system

What we’re posting

  • A/V expertise anyone can use

    Practical guidance, tips & techniques — useful whether or not they’re a client.

  • Thought leadership

    Mike’s perspective and the LUCI worldview, drawn from real conversations and experiences.

  • Capability reveals

    “What you didn’t know you could do with LUCI.” Show operators what is already possible on a platform they own.

  • Consolidation proof

    “The Shorter List”: one removed variable per post—racks, signage players, cost per change, or the refresh cycle itself.

  • Role-based capability

    The Field Activation Guide, broken into 24 use cases already grouped by role.

  • The Signal, broken into pieces

    Mine each issue for individual social posts, blog entries, and resource-center pieces instead of reposting it whole.

The Signal stays off social for now — issues carry named client stories. Once those are cleared or stripped, the newsletter becomes social-ready.

04 · First 30 days

Build the operating base

  • Two posts a week per channel, consistently
  • X live once Nick secures access
  • Follower and engagement growth
  • A reusable library of on-brand visuals, so production speeds up
  • Category language prospects can repeat back to us

05 · Long-term

Warm the market before outreach begins

  • An audience that already exists when outreach begins, so prospecting starts warm.

  • LUCI known as orchestration, not another A/V vendor.

  • Client stories published as approvals allow.
