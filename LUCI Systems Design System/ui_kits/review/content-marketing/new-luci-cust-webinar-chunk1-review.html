New LUCI upgrade campaign: emails 1–4

Theme: Putting the power of programming in your hands. Asset: LUCI Upgrade Overview. Private preview around late November, after beta. No release date appears anywhere. Yellow marks a placeholder.

Campaign strategy

Move current customers from informed consideration to attendance without repeating the same product pitch. Every email defines New LUCI in one line so it works for a cold reader. Email 1 opens on putting programming power in the customer’s hands, uses the LUCI Upgrade Overview before registration, and uses the retention claim once to reassure customers that the same LUCI team remains behind them. Email 2 makes the preview concrete with three takeaways and clearly labeled next steps. Email 3 is registrant-only logistics. Email 4 restores the product view for non-attendees. The tone moves from invitational to concrete to logistical to accommodating, with one earned human line in each send.
