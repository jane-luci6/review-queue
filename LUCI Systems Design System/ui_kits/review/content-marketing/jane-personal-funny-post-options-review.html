Option 2 Contrarian marketing take

Caption (as it reads in the feed)

If your campaign strategy sucks, AI just helps it suck at scale.

BasicAI can produce 40 headlines, six emails and a landing page before lunch.

That is not the same as knowing what any of them should say.

✅ Start with the audience, tension and argument.
👉 Use AI for options, pressure-testing and speed.
✅ Keep a human in the driver’s seat to decide what survives.

We don’t have a content-volume problem.

We have a judgment problem wearing an automation hat.

Where are you using AI to sharpen strategy—not just multiply output?

Contrarian marketing take with a dry punchline.
Visual idea

Option 3 Backpacking analogy

Caption (as it reads in the feed)

AI is excellent at carrying the pack. It should not pick the trail.

I’ve spent enough time backpacking out West to know that speed is useless when you’re headed the wrong way.

The same is true in marketing.

I use AI to research, organize, challenge and draft. What it doesn’t get to do is quietly choose the destination.

That’s why “human in the loop” feels too passive to me.

I want the human in the driver’s seat:

  • setting the strategy
  • defining the guardrails
  • questioning the output
  • making the final call

A faster wrong turn is still a wrong turn. It just arrives with better formatting.

How are you dividing the work between AI speed and human judgment?
