LUCI Systems · Services Order Form 02

Services

Scope of work.

LUCI Platform Licensing, IPTV Integration & Audio Integration Professional Services.

1. LUCI Licensing Update

Provide remote services to update and expand the existing LUCI platform licensing to include the additional system licenses as shown above. This upgrade will be performed in a manner that does not interrupt or impact ongoing system operation. All licensing updates will be validated upon completion to ensure full system functionality and availability.

2. Audio Deployment Coordination

Coordinate with the local contractor partner to plan and execute the deployment of new sound system hardware, including speakers and speaker cabling infrastructure. Services include configuration, programming, and commissioning of these devices, as well as full integration into the LUCI platform for centralized control, monitoring, and content distribution.

3. Video Integration

Coordinate with Boyd and associated project stakeholders to integrate new LG STB-6500 into the LUCI platform. STB-6500 will be installed and connected to owner provided and installed displays.

4. Control System Configuration & User Roles

Configure LUCI control workflows to support operations, including the creation of user roles, permissions, and operational presets. This includes defining control access levels, source selection behavior, and pre-configured system states tailored to use cases to enhance operational efficiency and ease of use.

LUCI Systems · Services Order Form 03

Services

Scope of work, continued.

Commissioning, documentation, and the assumptions this scope is priced against.

5. Testing & Commissioning

Perform remote and/or coordinated commissioning to validate proper operation of all newly integrated components, including licensing updates, IPTV distribution, encoder functionality, and video wall control within LUCI. Testing will confirm system stability, responsiveness, and alignment with operational requirements.

6. Documentation & Support Coordination

Provide updated system documentation reflecting licensing changes, IPTV channel configurations, and control system updates. Coordinate with on-site teams and client stakeholders to support deployment efforts and ensure a smooth transition to the updated system configuration.

Assumptions

This scope assumes all third-party hardware, including fiber optic network infrastructure required connectivity for IPTV and Audio distribution are provided and properly configured by others. Any on-site installation labor, hardware mounting, or third-party programming not explicitly stated is excluded from this scope.
