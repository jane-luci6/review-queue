The defining move

Yaamava’ was remodeling seven venues and adding 34 LED walls. Rather than let each one stand up another control environment, LUCI looped every wall into the platform as it came online, and moved audio and video onto the property network.

“What I love about LUCI’s approach is that they really focus on how to make the operations easier for the business.”

  • One place to send content

  • Less to maintain, not more

    Audio and video run over the property network — fewer processors to power and cool.
