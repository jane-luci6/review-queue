Review of scope

What this estimate covers.

The line items fund Phase 1 — 88 endpoints across video and audio, with the existing signage system carried forward and complex A/V reserved for a later phase.

Itemized by system — video and audio are in scope for Phase 1. Signage, lighting, and complex A/V are listed for context; they share the same platform and can be added in a later phase.

  • Video 50endpoints

    Maximum Reach, Limited Hardware (COM3000, Encoders, LG STBs or Hospitality Grade TVs).

  • Digital Signage Existing

    Only 1:1 players for menuboards, conference center, directional, and touch screens. The property keeps its existing Poppulo system for this phase.

  • Audio 38endpoints

    Automate, customize, and schedule independent channels and stations for sound on property, with presets.

  • Lighting Not included

    Ability to control the lighting system through the LUCI interface.

  • Complex A/V Future

    Design and install of LED, video walls, and similar. Consultative services for future projects; nothing additional quoted here.

Final scope, endpoint counts, and hardware selections are confirmed during the on-site walk that precedes the final quote.
