# CUST-WEBINAR-01 — Seminar invitation

- **Audience:** Operations and IT at current LUCI properties
- **Cycle:** Pre-launch webinar
- **Timing:** About 2–3 weeks before the webinar
- **Purpose:** Give customers an early look at New LUCI. Include the What’s New asset so they can see the features and customer benefits before deciding to attend.
- **CTA:** Review what is coming and register for the live preview.
- **Status:** draft r2 — for Jane review

## r2 changes
Opened on the campaign theme, added one warm invitation line, made the partnership concrete, used the client-retention claim once as reassurance, differentiated the subject-line moods, clarified New LUCI for a cold reader, and recounted the final body at 108 words.

## Strategy

### What we’re writing and why
A customer invitation that gives the LUCI Upgrade Overview enough weight to help a cold reader decide whether the private preview is useful. The email opens on the campaign theme, defines New LUCI in one line, and offers two sequential actions: scan the overview, then register.

### For whom
Operations should recognize more direct control over rooms and day-to-day changes. IT should recognize stronger visibility, access control, and support context. The copy keeps both groups in one shared customer conversation rather than splitting the message.

### Best way to reach them
Send from a named LUCI customer-facing lead through the marketing platform, with a human signature. Keep the body under 100 words, use short paragraphs, and send about 2–3 weeks before the preview. Put the overview link before registration so attendance is an informed choice.

### How to make impact while staying professional
Open on “Putting the power of programming in your hands,” then connect that authority to a concrete reassurance: the same LUCI team remains behind the customer. Use the retention claim only in that context, not as a headline or standalone boast.

### Primary message
See how New LUCI puts more programming power in your hands, with the same LUCI team behind you, then register for a private customer preview.
