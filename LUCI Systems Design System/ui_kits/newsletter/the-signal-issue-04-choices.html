Quick Tip — things people can do on LUCI now

  • Give one venue its own PIN, not the whole map

    Bingo (or any room) logs in and only sees its own endpoints. Confirm bingo as the example.

    Needs you

  • Name a new audio source without a ticket

    Plug into an input that’s already on the network, right-click in LUCI, label it. It’s on the map. Gain matching is the only reason to loop in an FDE.

    Ready

  • Put the TV’s audio on the room for a watch party

    That zone’s speakers play the game. No extra box. Lip-sync can drift on some sets; we’ll caveat if you pick it.

  • Kiosk login: a short list, not the map

    A conference room or cabana login sees only its screens and channels. Today’s permission pattern, not a new panel product.

    Needs you

  • Scan for offline before you walk

    Green is talking. Red is not. Fix volume and source from where you sit; walk only for a cable or a dead display.

Advice — how a live property should run

  • Divisible rooms are operating modes

    When the wall comes out, audio and video combine with the room. When it goes back, they split. You don’t lose that.

    Ready

  • Keep local control local

    The cabana or conference room gets the few controls it needs. The rest of the house stays coordinated. An operating rule, not a product.

    Ready

  • The systems already in the room stay

    LUCI coordinates them. It does not replace the A/V already bought.

    Ready

  • Expansion should join the house, not start a second console

    A new wall or room comes onto the interface already running the property.

    Ready

  • Use the plant that’s already in the floor

    If the old run still works, send the new stream back out on it. Don’t trench the floor to prove a point.

    Ready

  • Watch the calendar collision

    If two presets share the same TVs, the next one wins. Don’t stack a drawing on a daypart without a plan.

    Ready
