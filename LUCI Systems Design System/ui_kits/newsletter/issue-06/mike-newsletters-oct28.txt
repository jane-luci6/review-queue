November Message:
1. From a Vision to an Enterprise-Wide Solution
LUCI was founded with a mission to change how multimedia systems are designed, controlled, managed, and supported.
What started as an innovative approach to simplifying complex AV systems has evolved into a scalable solution adopted by some of the gaming industry's most recognized enterprises.
LUCI continues to demonstrate that complex multimedia environments can be standardized, centralized, and managed through one intuitive platform.
2. Enterprise Growth and Milestones
Boyd Gaming: LUCI has completed deployments at all but one of Boyd Gaming's enterprise properties within just a few short years.
Caesars Entertainment: LUCI is now deployed in more than 15 properties, with Caesars' own teams completing the installations, demonstrating the scalability and repeatability of the platform.
Choctaw and Osage Casinos: Each enterprise has adopted LUCI across five or more properties, creating consistent technology standards and centralized management capabilities.
Our growth from individual property deployments to enterprise-wide implementations demonstrates LUCI's ability to deliver consistent technology and support at scale.
3. A Client Retention Record We're Proud Of
Since LUCI's inception, we have never lost a client after onboarding.
Our commitment extends far beyond the initial deployment. We continue supporting, enhancing, and evolving our clients' multimedia environments throughout the relationship.
This milestone is a reflection of our ongoing commitment to service, reliability, and building lasting partnerships.
4. Changing the Traditional AV Integration Model
Historically, multimedia environments have relied on a collection of independent manufacturers, control systems, and technologies that become increasingly difficult to manage and support.
LUCI has challenged this approach by delivering a standardized, centrally managed solution that brings these technologies together.
Our model moves beyond the traditional project-to-project integration business, prioritizing long-term system performance, continuous modernization, and ongoing operational support.
We have demonstrated that technology can continue improving long after installation rather than becoming obsolete.
5. Continuing to Build for the Future
LUCI continues to invest in our software, engineering, integrations, and support capabilities.
Our expanding enterprise footprint has strengthened our ability to deliver scalable solutions across diverse gaming and hospitality environments.
As we prepare for the next generation of LUCI, our focus remains on making multimedia technology more powerful, more intuitive, and easier to manage than ever before.
