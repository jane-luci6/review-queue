4. Inside LUCI · Make the routine repeatable

OPEN · Piece pick: Jane

Repeated A/V work often begins as a list someone has to remember: make the same selections, move through the same sequence, check the same result, and return the room to the right state afterward. When staff rebuild that sequence by hand each time, small differences can become operating problems.

Scripting turns an approved sequence into one consistent action. The purpose is not to hide what the system is doing. It is to define the routine once so staff can execute it the same way each time, while the LUCI team can support a known process instead of diagnosing a different set of manual steps on every run.

A useful script starts with the real operating sequence: who initiates it, which A/V endpoints it affects, what must happen in what order, and what the room should look like when the sequence is complete. That gives the property a repeatable action tied to the way the space actually operates.

6. What’s coming · Putting the power of programming in your hands

OPEN · Piece pick: Jane

The new version of LUCI is in beta. Its purpose is larger than a new interface: it brings more of the day-to-day programming and operating work into direct reach of the people responsible for the property.

Most users will continue to run LUCI on an iPad, while administrators complete setup on a computer. The distinction matters. Operators need focused control in the room; administrators need the tools to define what each person can see and do. The beta brings both closer to the work without weakening the support relationship behind it.

The new version is organized around three forms of control:

  • Greater control of the room. Venue panels can present the controls, sources, presets, and venues approved for a specific space, keeping daily operation focused while administrative setup stays with the right people.
  • Flexible control of your view. Live map flexibility lets administrators load the property’s floor plans, keep them current, and return users to the relevant map position across sessions and devices.
  • Informed control of your operation. Audit trails bring actions into one record that can be traced by time, user, device, and trigger, giving teams a clearer path from an operating question to the activity behind it.

These features put more direct authority with the client, but they do not move LUCI out of the relationship. The LUCI team remains involved in planning, setup, monitoring, diagnosis, support, and continued refinement. Your team can handle more of the routine work while the same accountable team stays behind the platform.

The LUCI Upgrade Guide brings the beta’s pillars, features, and upgrade path together in one place. We will share it directly as the beta progresses.
