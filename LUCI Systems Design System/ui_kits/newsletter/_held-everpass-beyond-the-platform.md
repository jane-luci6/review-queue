# HELD — "Beyond the platform" (EverPass / NFL Sunday Ticket)

Pulled from **The Signal, Issue 02 — July 2026** before publishing.
Held on 2026-07-10 at Jane's call.

## Why it was held

The story reads positive, but it's the first public mention of LUCI's
EverPass integration. A few months ago we emailed all customers telling them
to schedule a visit to enable the change (box + programming). The customers
who booked us now have NFL Sunday Ticket in LUCI; the ones who didn't will
**not** have it in August. Publishing this could trigger a flood of "wait, we
need this!" inbound the team doesn't have capacity to handle right now.

We still want to leverage the **proactive partnership** message — just not via
this specific, time-sensitive example in the July issue.

## When to revive

- After the customers who haven't enabled EverPass have been worked through
  (so the inbound is manageable), **or**
- Re-frame the example so it doesn't name EverPass / NFL Sunday Ticket and
  can't trigger the flood — abstract it to "proactive integration" with a
  non-urgent example, **or**
- Use it as the basis for a standalone case study / sales asset rather than a
  newsletter blurb.

## The copy (as written)

**Kicker:** Beyond the platform
**Title:** Giving you what you need before you need it

This fall, NFL Sunday Ticket moves entirely to streaming, with a single
authorized home for commercial venues: EverPass. For any property with a
sportsbook, that's a real transition — new hardware, new bandwidth, a new
system to run on game day.

We saw it coming. LUCI built direct, cloud-level access to EverPass before the
switch was forced — one of only a handful of companies in the country to do
it, authenticating straight to their cloud rather than passing video through.
So when the deadline came, the properties on our calendar weren't facing a
project. The integration was finished.

And it works the way everything else in LUCI does: EverPass channels behave
like the DirecTV sources always have — same map, same channel ring, tunable to
any endpoint. Not a new system to learn, just another source to tune. Most
were running it well before opening weekend.

We're here every step of the way — and the best part of that is often the step
you never see, the work we do before you'd have thought to ask for it. That's
the commitment: same team, same engineers, looking around the next corner so
you don't have to. That's what partnership with LUCI looks like: the tomorrow
shows up, and you're already on the right side of it.
