## Para A

Standardize the parts your team relies on every day: who can control what, how devices and spaces are named, and what happens when a command is sent. When those rules stay consistent, people can move from one part of the property to another without learning a different operating method each time.

## Para B

Adapt to what each space needs. A sportsbook, ballroom, and casino floor do different jobs, and the equipment inside them will change over time. Those differences should fit within the standard—not create another process for the team to manage.
