# The Signal — Issue 04 ActiveCampaign teaser copy

## Email headline

A 106-foot field story is inside

## Intro body

We’re excited to take you inside Aliante’s sportsbook, where LUCI brought a 106' x 20' curved LED wall, the bar ticker, and the whole room onto the platform the property already runs. It’s a field story about adding something immense without adding another way to operate it.

That same question carries through the issue: what should stay consistent, and what should adapt to the person, room, or job? A New LUCI map follows your point of view, the Quick Tip shows how endpoint names and service notes leave a useful record for the next technician, and the Field Activation Guide gives every role a place to start. Mike closes with why the operation—not the hardware—is what should stay standard.
