## Onscreen type (supers only)

**Hierarchy**

| Role | Face | Color | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Title line 1 (S1) | Syncopate 700 | White | `Aliante Casino + Hotel` — short display only; one line; do not wrap |
| Title line 2 (S1) | Space Grotesk 700 | White (or mint `#68E3BE` at low emphasis) | `A sportsbook renovation with LUCI Systems` |
| All other supers | Space Grotesk 700 | White | Running cards; ~10-word ceiling already in copy |
| End card | Space Grotesk 700 | White | Held ~2.5–3s on freeze per paper edit |

**Placement + readability**

- Prefer **lower third** for body cards; **center stack** only for the two title lines and the end card.
- Soft **navy scrim** (`#0A161C` at ~40–60% opacity, gradient edge) behind type when footage is busy. No boxed mint panels, no thick bars, no circuit texture.
- One card on screen at a time. Respect paper-edit in/out times; fade in/out ~8–12 frames is enough.
- Leave safe margins (~8–10% from edges). Do not cover faces or the LED wall’s main content when avoidable.
- No Syncopate on any mid-video sentence card.
