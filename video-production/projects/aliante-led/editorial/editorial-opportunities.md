## Recommended integrated direction — "The Wall, Orchestrated"

**Jane's editorial direction (2026-09-03):** The curved LED wall should be the primary visual backbone of the Aliante case study because it has the strongest and most extensive visual evidence, its scale is immediately understandable, the installation process is well documented, and the finished wall provides a strong visual payoff.

However, the case study is NOT fundamentally a construction video or an LED-wall-installation story. The larger LUCI message is: a visual installation this large and complex can ultimately be managed simply through LUCI — alongside the rest of the casino's multimedia environment. The wall provides the visual spectacle and project narrative. LUCI's orchestration and simplification provide the business/product meaning.

| **2. Primary LUCI message — complexity made simple** | The conceptual point of the case study | The finished installation is physically and technically substantial, but LUCI turns its operation into something dramatically simpler. Legacy projector removal (20+ projectors) and LUCI hardware reinforce that simplification. It is available as a supporting device within the wall narrative. It is not the full video concept and not necessarily the structural backbone. |

### Why this integrated direction works

1. **The wall carries the video visually.** The strongest footage in the library is the completed wall (V0036, V0040, I0065) and the installation process (V0028, V0027). No other component has comparable visual evidence. Making the wall the backbone ensures the video is visually compelling throughout.

2. **LUCI's meaning lands as the payoff, not the premise.** The viewer sees the spectacle first (the wall), then learns that this enormous installation is managed simply through one platform alongside everything else. The simplification is the conceptual reward for watching the build.

3. **The property-wide context expands the story without losing focus.** After the wall and the simplification, the story zooms out to show that this is one part of a much larger environment — 266 displays, 89 audio zones, 98 digital signage endpoints. The B-Roll property tour (V0002, V0004, V0010, V0014, V0016) provides the visual evidence for this expansion.

4. **Rack consolidation is a major visual proof point of the simplification message — not peripheral supporting proof.** The before/after contrast between legacy infrastructure (V0048 dusty decommissioned projectors, V0053 decommissioned racks) and the completed LUCI rack environment (V0051, I0078, V0052) is one of the clearest ways to make "complexity made simple" visible. The 15-racks-to-2 consolidation makes subtraction concrete and verifiable. This is where the wall's business meaning becomes visible: behind the spectacle, LUCI removed 15 racks and 20+ projectors and replaced them with 2 LUCI racks. The rack consolidation serves the simplification message (Layer 2) rather than being a separate minor layer.

5. **The 5-photo progression is available but not required.** If the wall narrative needs a visual device to show the build over time, the matched-viewpoint sequence is ready. If the story works better without it (e.g., opening on the finished wall and rewinding via video footage instead), the sequence can be omitted or used as a brief interlude. The decision belongs to the next stage.

### What the available media and evidence cannot adequately support

- **No LUCI interface/iPad footage.** The story cannot show HOW LUCI controls the wall. The simplification message must be made through onscreen text, the breadth of what's shown, and the contrast between old and new infrastructure — not through a product demonstration.
- **No customer voice.** All speakers are LUCI team members. V0024/V0026 are excluded as A-roll per Jane's decision. The story must be told through visuals, onscreen text, and music — not through interview audio.
- **No true-before VIDEO of the old system operating.** V0048 shows decommissioned projectors in storage, not the old sportsbook running. However, **true-before still photography now exists** (I0090, I0091, I0092 — added 2026-09-04) showing the old sportsbook in operation with the legacy projector display wall. The case study can now establish an old-sportsbook → renovation → new-sportsbook progression using actual before imagery. No true-before video exists — only still photography.
- **No wall-configuration switching footage.** The six configurations and the tap-a-box workflow cannot be shown. They must be described through onscreen text if used at all.

This direction will likely require: onscreen text for key stats and the simplification message; music-led storytelling (no VO available); careful editing to bridge the visual quality gap between hero wall footage (VQ 4-5) and rack room footage (VQ 2-4); and a structural decision about whether to open on the finished wall and rewind into the build, or build toward the reveal. ## Evaluation of components within the integrated direction

| Component | Role in integrated story | Visual evidence strength | Factual evidence strength | Risk if over-emphasized |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A — The Wall** | Primary visual backbone | 5/5 — strongest footage in the library | 4/5 — strong install facts | If it dominates, the video becomes a construction timelapse, not a platform story |
| **B — The Consolidation** | Major visual proof point of simplification (within Layer 2) | 3/5 — rack room footage is lower quality, but the before/after contrast is one of the clearest ways to make "complexity made simple" visible | 5/5 — strongest factual story (15 → 2, 20+ projectors) | If it dominates, the video becomes "the rack consolidation video" and loses the wall's visual spectacle |
| **C — The Property** | Secondary expansion | 3/5 — B-Roll is context, not spectacle | 5/5 — strongest property-wide facts | If it dominates, the video becomes a property tour without a thesis |
| **D — The Curve** | Adds specificity to the wall story | 4/5 — strong installation + hero reveals | 4/5 — strong precision facts | If it dominates, the video becomes a single visual trick stretched too thin |

**The integrated direction resolves the major tradeoff:** Component A has the best visual evidence but the weakest positioning fit. Component B has the best factual evidence and positioning fit but weaker visuals. By making A the backbone and B a major visual proof point of the simplification message (Layer 2), the story gets A's visual spectacle AND B's product meaning — with the rack consolidation making "complexity made simple" visible rather than abstract. Component C expands the scope without losing focus. Component D adds credibility to the wall story without becoming the whole story.
