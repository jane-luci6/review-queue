## Editorial decisions / tradeoffs

- **Omitted approved line `The 20+ projectors were specifically part of the old sportsbook wall system.` (section 3).** Reason: the card is 12 words, over the ~10-word ceiling, and the section's order (old sportsbook stills to old projectors removed to new wall) already communicates old-wall-out / new-wall-in without it. The constraint (do not imply the projectors were infrastructure for the new LED wall) is carried by sequencing, not by a card. Flagged here rather than silently replaced, per the copy rule.

- **Held the LUCI Systems / LUCI platform distinction to section 5.** The opening (section 1) names Aliante + LUCI Systems as build partner only; the platform is not introduced until section 5. This deliberately avoids blurring the two LUCIs at the open, at the cost of delaying the full relationship until ~0:42.

- **Split the wall across three sections (4, 7, 9, 11) instead of one hero reveal.** The wall is the visual backbone but the full hero payoff is reserved for section 9; section 4 uses a wide establishing shot (V0041) and section 7 uses a different hero (V0036) so the ending (V0040) is not the same shot as the turn. Tradeoff: the wall appears several times, which risks redundancy; mitigated by using a different angle/shot each time and by leaving the payoff and ending largely text-free so the repeats feel like bookends, not reruns.

- **Used V0049 before V0050 in the rack section.** V0050 is the stronger pure AFTER proof (per the brief), but V0049 carries the empty-footprint to populated-rack transition, which makes the 15 to 2 reduction visible in one clip. Tradeoff: the stronger shot is second, not first; accepted because the transition arc carries more narrative weight than the cleaner static proof, and V0050 lands the operational after as the section's final image.

- **Reused V0002 (casino floor) at both the open (section 1) and the property-wide expansion (section 10).** Intentional: the reuse signals the camera pulling back out to the whole property at section 10. Tradeoff: the same shot appears twice; mitigated by using a different range (0:01.6-0:08.0 at the open vs 0:01.6-0:10.0 at section 10) and a different role (establish Aliante vs zoom outward).

- **No VO, no interview audio.** Per the approved format and the no-customer-interview constraint. The simplification message is carried by the rack contrast (section 8) and the turn card (section 7), not by a narrator.

## Major evidence limitation affecting this edit

- **No true-before VIDEO of the old sportsbook operating.** Only true-before still photography exists (I0090, I0091, I0092). Section 3 therefore holds three static before-images rather than motion footage of the old room. This is the single largest evidence limitation shaping the edit: the before-sequence cannot use a camera move through the old sportsbook, only held stills. The renovation-to-new-design transition is carried by cutting from the before-stills to the decommissioned-projector video (V0048) to the new-wall reveal (section 4).

No other evidence gap materially affected the structure. The rack consolidation, the build, and the finished hero all have sufficient main-rack / Tier-1 evidence to support their sections as planned.
