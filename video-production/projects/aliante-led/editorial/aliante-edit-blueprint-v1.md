## Rough Cut 2 Diagnosis

### What already works

- The opening uses casino-floor footage for casino-size claims. It establishes Aliante as a substantial North Las Vegas casino and resort without borrowing meaning from generic lobby footage.
- The old sportsbook photographs are legitimate before-state evidence. The projector-removal visual extends that evidence from the room into the legacy infrastructure.
- The cut includes a substantial moving construction section, places the LUCI turn in racks and infrastructure, and gives rack consolidation real weight.
- The finished wall is withheld until after the rack proof, then receives a long, copy-free reveal. The ticker follows it rather than pre-empting it.
- The matched five-photo progression is near the end, not substituted for the moving build.
- The live sportsbook close and dedicated LUCI end card resolve the story cleanly.

### Where the story breaks

1. **The renovation loses forward motion immediately after the before state.** The old sportsbook and legacy projectors establish a clear problem. The next three shots leave the sportsbook for three different property locations. The cards explain why LUCI was selected, but the visuals read as a new property montage rather than evidence in the same causal chain.

2. **The trusted-partner chapter is three stacked facts, not one argument.** “Aliante's A/V already ran on the LUCI platform,” “So LUCI Systems was the obvious build partner,” and “For the wall and ticker” are logically connected. In RC2, each line arrives over a different environment, so the edit resets the subject three times. The chapter needs one visual idea: LUCI was already embedded in active A/V environments across the same property; therefore the sportsbook renovation extended an existing operating relationship.

3. **The construction footage shows activity but not a deliberate progression.** The sequence has scale, lifts, workers, wall structure, and ticker structure, but the clips currently sit beside one another as examples. Their order should make the work accumulate: room and footprint → structural scale → labor → panel placement and alignment → ticker precision → infrastructure consequence.

4. **The LUCI turn arrives as a technical chapter instead of the answer to the build.** Moving from construction into racks is directionally right. The missing logic is: a build this substantial creates an operating burden; LUCI is how that environment becomes simple to run. The first infrastructure image must bridge into the rack proof without showing the consolidated answer before the 15 → 13 empty → 2 progression.

5. **The broader-property section has the right destination but weak copy-to-visual proof.** “And the sportsbook is only part of it” belongs with lobby-led property footage. But the following scale line — “266 displays. 89 zones. 7+ technologies.” — is carried mostly by elegant lobby views that do not consistently show displays, zones, or A/V operation. The result feels like detached resort B-roll. Start with the lobby to widen the world, then use property footage where active A/V endpoints are visible for the scale and orchestration claims. If that category does not exist, it is a visual gap; generic decor should not fill it.

6. **The matched-photo recap reads as a late return to construction unless its function is explicit.** Its placement is correct, but the edit shifts from the current property-wide environment back to construction with no framing. The five images should behave as one compressed transformation gesture — a brief visual recollection after the full scope has been established — then release directly into the sportsbook alive with guests.

### Copy and reveal diagnosis

- The casino scale cards match casino-floor visuals and should remain there.
- The 14,200-square-foot sportsbook and 20+ legacy projector lines are supported by the before room and projector visuals. They are project-scope facts, not invented outcomes.
- The build card correctly says effort and scale, not generic complexity.
- The rack cards form the strongest proof chain in the cut. Their visual order must make the denominator and subtraction unmistakable.
- No substantial finished wall or ticker appears early. That discipline should remain.
- The wall reveal is earned and has room to breathe. No copy should be added to it.
- The ticker card belongs only after the wall has been seen.
- The approved game-day close and dedicated end card should remain unchanged.

## Scene-by-Scene Editorial Blueprint

### Beat 1 — Name the property and the project

- **Story purpose:** Identify Aliante Casino + Hotel and establish that this is a sportsbook renovation case study, not a generic LUCI reel.
- **Onscreen copy:** **Locked:** `Aliante Casino + Hotel` followed by `A sportsbook renovation with LUCI Systems`
- **Required visual meaning:** A confident casino-floor arrival with recognizable scale, depth, and active gaming-floor context.
- **Why the visual belongs:** The property name and project title need a real sense of place before the film argues scope or transformation.
- **Transition logic:** Once the viewer knows where the story is and what changed, the next beat can prove how substantial the property is.
- **Reveal status:** Finished wall and ticker remain hidden.
- **Approximate duration:** 6 seconds.

### Beat 2 — Establish Aliante's scale

- **Story purpose:** Show that the sportsbook sits inside a substantial North Las Vegas casino and resort.
- **Onscreen copy:** **Locked:** `100,000+ square feet of gaming.` followed by `Across more than 40 acres.`
- **Required visual meaning:** Casino-floor views only — gaming positions, long sightlines, and the visible footprint of a large operating property.
- **Why the visual belongs:** These claims have casino-floor meaning; the images must prove gaming scale rather than merely show attractive resort interiors.
- **Transition logic:** The film starts wide with the property, then narrows from that scale to one major venue inside it.
- **Reveal status:** Finished wall and ticker remain hidden.
- **Approximate duration:** 8 seconds.

### Beat 3 — Enter the sportsbook renovation

- **Story purpose:** Turn the film from property context into the specific project.
- **Onscreen copy:** No additional copy. Let the physical renovation threshold carry the turn.
- **Required visual meaning:** A real renovation marker or barrier that clearly identifies the sportsbook as the place about to change.
- **Why the visual belongs:** It converts the opening scale into a specific promise: this is where the case study begins.
- **Transition logic:** The viewer has arrived at the sportsbook; the next beat answers what the room was before the renovation.
- **Reveal status:** Finished wall and ticker remain hidden.
- **Approximate duration:** 3–4 seconds.

### Beat 4 — Show the real previous sportsbook

- **Story purpose:** Establish the legitimate before state and the scope of the legacy system being removed.
- **Onscreen copy:** **Locked:** `Aliante set out to renovate its 14,200-square-foot sportsbook.` followed by `The renovation removed more than 20 legacy projectors.`
- **Required visual meaning:** The true previous sportsbook first, then its legacy projection equipment. The room should be readable before the edit moves into the hardware.
- **Why the visual belongs:** The viewer needs to see both the former guest environment and one concrete part of the system behind it.
- **Transition logic:** The old room establishes what had to change. The next question is why LUCI was already positioned to take on that change.
- **Reveal status:** Finished wall and ticker remain hidden.
- **Approximate duration:** 9–10 seconds.

### Beat 5 — Establish LUCI as the embedded property partner

- **Story purpose:** Show that the sportsbook was an extension of an existing property-wide A/V relationship, not a first encounter.
- **Onscreen copy:** **Locked:** `Aliante's A/V already ran on the LUCI platform.` → `So LUCI Systems was the obvious build partner.` → `For the wall and ticker.`
- **Required visual meaning:** A compact, coherent run of active A/V environments elsewhere on the same property. Favor visible displays or controlled endpoints and consistent visual movement. Reserve the strongest lobby expansion footage for Beat 13.
- **Why the visual belongs:** Existing operational reach is the evidence for trust; three unrelated beauty shots are not.
- **Transition logic:** LUCI already operated across Aliante; therefore the new sportsbook became the next substantial environment to build and bring into that standard.
- **Reveal status:** Finished sportsbook wall and ticker remain hidden. Existing property displays may appear, but nothing that reads as the completed sportsbook reveal.
- **Approximate duration:** 10–11 seconds.

### Beat 6 — Make the build accumulate

- **Story purpose:** Communicate the effort, physical scale, labor, and precision required to build the new experience.
- **Onscreen copy:** **Locked:** `The hard part is building at this scale.` Use the card once near the beginning of the sequence, then let the work continue without stacked claims.
- **Required visual meaning:** A moving construction progression with distinct jobs: the room and footprint; wall or ticker structure; crews and lifts; panel placement; alignment, cabling, or another visible precision task. This is not the five matched stills.
- **Why the visual belongs:** The audience should feel the build becoming more exact and consequential, not watch a collection of interchangeable construction clips.
- **Transition logic:** The physical undertaking creates the next problem: after this much equipment and labor, how does the property operate the finished environment without inheriting the same scale of complexity?
- **Reveal status:** The completed wall and completed ticker remain hidden. Unfinished structure, frames, panels, and in-progress installation are allowed.
- **Approximate duration:** 21–24 seconds.

### Beat 7 — Turn from construction to operation

- **Story purpose:** State the central operating argument: LUCI makes the substantial finished system simple to run.
- **Onscreen copy:** **Locked:** `LUCI makes running it the easy part.`
- **Required visual meaning:** A visual move from the room into infrastructure — entry into the rack environment, cabling, processing, or system detail — without yet showing the final two-rack answer.
- **Why the visual belongs:** The film is not leaving the build for an unrelated technical aside; it is showing where the operational burden is resolved.
- **Transition logic:** The statement makes a promise. The following rack sequence proves it with a before, a subtraction, and a final standard.
- **Reveal status:** Finished wall and ticker remain hidden. Final consolidated rack count should not be visually disclosed yet.
- **Approximate duration:** 4–5 seconds.

### Beat 8 — Set the rack denominator

- **Story purpose:** Make the legacy infrastructure large enough for the consolidation result to carry weight.
- **Onscreen copy:** **Locked:** `Aliante's legacy system filled 15 racks.`
- **Required visual meaning:** Clearly dense legacy racks and cabling. The image must read as the former condition, not the current LUCI standard.
- **Why the visual belongs:** Fifteen is the denominator. Without a legible before state, the later empty racks and two-rack result become unsupported numbers.
- **Transition logic:** Once the viewer sees what filled the room, the next beat can show what disappeared.
- **Reveal status:** Finished wall and ticker remain hidden.
- **Approximate duration:** 5 seconds.

### Beat 9 — Show the subtraction

- **Story purpose:** Turn simplification into visible proof.
- **Onscreen copy:** **Locked:** `13 now sit empty.`
- **Required visual meaning:** A sustained, unmistakable view of empty rack bays or an empty rack aisle. Do not cut away before the subtraction registers.
- **Why the visual belongs:** Empty infrastructure is the strongest visual proof that LUCI removed operating burden rather than adding another system beside it.
- **Transition logic:** Thirteen empty racks create the arithmetic question the next beat answers: what remains?
- **Reveal status:** Finished wall and ticker remain hidden.
- **Approximate duration:** 6–7 seconds.

### Beat 10 — Reveal the current two-rack standard

- **Story purpose:** Complete the 15 → 13 empty → 2 proof chain and show the infrastructure that runs the sportsbook.
- **Onscreen copy:** **Locked:** `With LUCI, everything now runs from 2.`
- **Required visual meaning:** Two active LUCI racks together first, followed by enough operational detail to make the current standard credible. The pair must be visually countable.
- **Why the visual belongs:** This is the proof behind “easy to run,” not a product beauty shot.
- **Transition logic:** The film has now explained how a major build becomes operationally smaller. Only then can it reveal what those two racks run.
- **Reveal status:** Infrastructure is intentionally revealed; finished wall and ticker remain hidden until the next beat.
- **Approximate duration:** 8–9 seconds.

### Beat 11 — Earn the finished wall

- **Story purpose:** Deliver the primary visual payoff after the viewer understands the property, the former room, the work, and the simplified operating system.
- **Onscreen copy:** No copy.
- **Required visual meaning:** The completed sportsbook wall in its full environment, with sustained movement and enough visual breadth to understand its scale. Do not begin on an isolated screen detail.
- **Why the visual belongs:** The wall is the result of every prior beat. Silence from cards lets the payoff register as an experience rather than another claim.
- **Transition logic:** The wall establishes the centerpiece; the next beat reveals the secondary feature that wraps the room.
- **Reveal status:** Finished wall is intentionally revealed. Ticker remains held until Beat 12.
- **Approximate duration:** 15–16 seconds.

### Beat 12 — Reveal the ticker

- **Story purpose:** Extend the finished sportsbook reveal from the wall to the surrounding guest experience.
- **Onscreen copy:** **Locked:** `A 150-foot LED ticker wraps the sportsbook bar.`
- **Required visual meaning:** The completed ticker in relation to the sportsbook bar, with enough context to read “wraps” rather than a tight, contextless strip.
- **Why the visual belongs:** The copy names a spatial relationship, so the visual must show that relationship.
- **Transition logic:** With both signature sportsbook elements revealed, the film can widen back out and show that they belong to a larger LUCI-operated property.
- **Reveal status:** Ticker is intentionally revealed after the wall.
- **Approximate duration:** 7 seconds.

### Beat 13 — Expand to the property-wide LUCI environment

- **Story purpose:** Reframe the sportsbook as the newest part of a broader operating environment across Aliante.
- **Onscreen copy:** **Locked:** `And the sportsbook is only part of it.` → `266 displays. 89 zones. 7+ technologies.` → `All orchestrated through LUCI.`
- **Required visual meaning:** Begin with lobby footage so the expansion feels like moving outward from the sportsbook into the resort. Then show active displays, distinct A/V environments, or endpoints elsewhere on the property under the scale and orchestration cards. Keep a consistent sense of one property, not a tourism montage.
- **Why the visual belongs:** The lobby provides the spatial expansion; endpoint-bearing footage proves the numbers and the property-wide operating claim.
- **Transition logic:** The opening established a large property. This beat closes that loop by showing the completed sportsbook joining the same environment LUCI already operates. The next beat briefly compresses the sportsbook's physical transformation before the final live scene.
- **Reveal status:** Finished wall and ticker are already revealed and may appear only if needed as a visual bridge out of the sportsbook.
- **Approximate duration:** 16–18 seconds.
- **Visual gap:** If the available broader-property footage does not visibly support displays, zones, or active A/V operation, keep the lobby-led opener but flag the scale-card coverage for sourcing. Do not substitute generic Aliante decor.

### Beat 14 — Compress the transformation into one recap

- **Story purpose:** Recap the physical journey from construction to completed wall without restarting the build story.
- **Onscreen copy:** No copy.
- **Required visual meaning:** The five matched progress photographs in chronological order, sharing a clear point of view and ending at completion. Treat them as one short visual sentence.
- **Why the visual belongs:** Repeated framing lets the viewer see transformation instantly; the photos summarize what the moving construction sequence made them feel.
- **Transition logic:** The property-wide chapter establishes the present operating scope. This brief recollection closes the sportsbook transformation, then hands directly to the completed room in use.
- **Reveal status:** Completed wall has already been revealed. The final photo may show it; this is recap, not a second reveal.
- **Approximate duration:** 5–6 seconds.

### Beat 15 — Finish with the sportsbook alive

- **Story purpose:** Move from completed infrastructure to the guest-ready operating result.
- **Onscreen copy:** Hold the first live view without copy, then use the **approved locked close:** `The wall, the ticker, and LUCI are ready for game day.`
- **Required visual meaning:** The completed sportsbook active with content and people, followed by a strong operational view where wall, ticker, and room read together.
- **Why the visual belongs:** The case study ends with the environment doing its job, not with empty architecture or construction.
- **Transition logic:** The transformation recap resolves into the living result; the approved line then hands the case study to LUCI's institutional end card.
- **Reveal status:** Wall and ticker are fully revealed and operational.
- **Approximate duration:** 8–10 seconds.

### Beat 16 — Dedicated LUCI end card

- **Story purpose:** Close on the brand position after the case-study proof is complete.
- **Onscreen copy:** **Locked:** `The Orchestration Engine for Enterprise Multimedia`
- **Required visual meaning:** Dedicated LUCI end card, separate from footage.
- **Why the visual belongs:** The case study earns the positioning line; the brand card should not compete with the final sportsbook image.
- **Transition logic:** End.
- **Reveal status:** Not applicable; no additional project reveal.
- **Approximate duration:** 3–4 seconds.
