Swigs at Osage Casino Hotel Ponca City, Oklahoma · [second venue TBD]

Osage operates properties across Oklahoma. LUCI has worked with Osage for at least four years, and this year the partnership returned to Ponca City: first for another venue in the expansion, then for Swigs, the property’s new bar and live entertainment space.

At Swigs, the room needed to move easily from everyday bar service to sports and live entertainment. LUCI delivered integrated A/V and control around a nearly 20-foot display: 60 cabinets and 480 panels follow the architecture across three angled planes while reading as one continuous canvas.

Four operational presets let the property shift the wall between large-format content, multi-source television, and a live-performance backdrop. Two new audio zones support the bar and stage, while LUCI brings the new video and audio endpoints into the same centralized control environment as the property’s existing technology. A four-person onsite team, backed by home-office support, completed the work in 25.5 total working hours.

For guests, the technical precision disappears into a venue that can change with the moment. For Osage, Swigs carries an established relationship forward: a new space integrated into the LUCI environment already operating at the property.
