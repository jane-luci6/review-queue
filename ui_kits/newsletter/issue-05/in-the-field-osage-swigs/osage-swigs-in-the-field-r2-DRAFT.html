Years together, with more room to move

Osage Casino Hotel — Ponca City Original install · Ballroom · Swizzle · Swigs

The relationship at Osage Casino Hotel in Ponca City began years ago with an original LUCI installation. Since then, the property has brought more of its operation onto the same control environment—first the ballroom, then two venue chapters this year: Swizzle followed by Swigs.

Each return built on what was already operating. After the original installation, LUCI brought the ballroom onto the platform. Swizzle followed as the first of the two recent venue phases. Swigs came next, extending the same working relationship into the property’s new bar and live entertainment space.

At Swigs, 60 LED cabinets and 480 panels form one continuous canvas across three angled planes. Four presets shift the display between large-format content, multi-source television, and a live-performance backdrop. Two new audio zones support the bar and stage, while LUCI integrates the new video and audio endpoints alongside the property’s existing technology. A four-person onsite team, backed by home-office support, completed the work in 25.5 total working hours.

Swigs is the latest chapter, not the whole story. The value is continuity: each new room joins an established control environment, and the same working relationship carries into the next phase.
