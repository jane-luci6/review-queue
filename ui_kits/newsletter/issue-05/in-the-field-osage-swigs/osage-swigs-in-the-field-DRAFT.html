In the field

One wall, more ways to run the night

Swigs at Osage Casino Hotel Ponca City, Oklahoma

Inside Osage Casino Hotel in Ponca City, Oklahoma, Swigs is the property’s new bar and live entertainment venue. LUCI helped bring it online with a three-plane LED wall, two new audio zones, and centralized control.

The nearly 20-foot-wide, 1.56mm-pitch CoB display follows the room instead of flattening it: a 7′ 10½″ center section behind the stage, with two 5′ 10¾″ wings angled toward the audience. Sixty cabinets and 480 panels had to meet cleanly at each turn, then map as one continuous 3840 × 1296 canvas.

  • 3 planesOne continuous wall
  • 480 panelsAcross 60 cabinets
  • 25.5 hoursTotal working time

The onsite team completed installation-to-finish work in 25.5 working hours, against four full working days on the schedule. Four operational presets now move the wall from large-format marketing to multi-source television and a live-performance backdrop. LUCI also brings the new A/V endpoints and audio zones into centralized control alongside the property’s existing technology.

For guests, the technical precision disappears into the room. Swigs can shift from drinks and the game to live music without asking one screen to do only one job.
