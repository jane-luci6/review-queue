Standing workflow (applies to EVERY content and layout item below)

Jane set this rule after the meeting: nobody implements a change on the Upgrade Guide (.37), the What's New two-pager, the website/Platform pages, emails, or video until Jane approves the proposed change.

  1. Draft: The assignee writes the proposed edit (copy, layout, or capture) as a before/after or mock.
  2. Jane review: The assignee sends the draft to Jane. Jane approves, edits, or rejects it.
  3. Implement: The assignee makes the change only after Jane approves it.
  4. Close: The assignee tells Cornelius, and Cornelius marks the item done.

Status key

  • LOCKED: Jane and Richard agreed the exact change in the meeting (for example, delete X or replace X with Y). The assignee still sends the edit to Jane for a quick confirm before publishing.
  • NEEDS JANE APPROVAL OF DRAFT BEFORE IMPLEMENT: The meeting agreed only a direction. Someone has to write new wording, layout, or a visual, and Jane approves it before anyone implements it.
  • BLOCKED: The item waits on a product fix or a demo deploy. Status is shown with the blocker.

Destination shorthand: UG = Upgrade Guide (on .37) · 2P = What's New two-pager · WEB = website/Platform · VIS = screenshots/video · EML = emails · OTHER
