# New LUCI Campaign Strategy

Enterprise properties already operate more A/V systems than any one team should have to manage separately. New LUCI sits above the A/V systems already in the building, bringing them into one operating environment without replacing the technology each property has already invested in.

**The Orchestration Engine for Enterprise Multimedia.**

**One interface to control, automate, and execute the entire guest experience.**

Platform and partnership as one offering.

The campaign addresses three audiences in sequence. Existing customers come first, with New LUCI introduced as a beta property by property. Warm leads follow. Demand generation comes later and continues to lead with LUCI's established offer, not New LUCI. This is not a public launch, and public LinkedIn communication waits until LUCI is ready to speak beyond the customer base.

## 1. Existing customers

### Audience

Operations and IT teams at current LUCI properties.

### Strategy

Current customers receive the new capabilities first, on a timeline set with each property and with a clear path from the LUCI environment they operate today. The beta proceeds one property at a time.

### Story

New LUCI extends A/V control to the people and spaces where operations happen while the property's existing A/V systems remain in place. Teams can prepare the next room configuration without changing what is live. Every action records who initiated it, when it happened, and what triggered it. Support requests can begin from the affected device, incident, or error, with the relevant context attached. The interface can reflect the property's brand and the status conventions its operators already use.

Four primary capabilities establish this story. The customizable interface provides the visual introduction.

### Assets and sequence

1. **What's New** shows a property already using LUCI what changed and why it matters.
2. **Upgrade guide** explains what the property can expect when it chooses to join the beta.
3. **IT companion** shows on-site IT how New LUCI fits above the property's existing A/V environment.
4. **Customer email, field talking points, and The Signal** introduce and support the beta.

Public LinkedIn communication waits until LUCI is ready to discuss New LUCI outside the customer base.

### Primary capabilities

- **Venue panels.** A tablet in the space gives the person operating that space the controls appropriate to the venue—an events manager in a ballroom or a guest at a pool cabana. The panel includes that venue's endpoints and only the controls permitted by the administrator. The person using the panel does not see the main LUCI application.
- **Staging.** Teams can build the next configuration while the current one continues to run. Nothing in the room changes until **Apply** is selected. A staged configuration can be saved as a preset; assigning a time turns it into a scheduled preset.
- **Audit trails and in-product support.** Every action is recorded with the person, time, and trigger associated with it. A support request can be raised from the device, incident, or error itself, and arrives with the relevant context and logs attached. Escalation remains a deliberate decision by an authorized person.
- **Customizable interface.** The sign-in and splash screens can use the property's photography and brand marks. The interface can be light or dark to match the property. Endpoint status colors follow the conventions operators already know.

### Secondary capabilities

- Audio group control
- A live map that reflects the property floor
- The ability to add any endpoint directly in LUCI
- Email, PIN, or Entra sign-in
- Video wall layout synchronization from the processor
- A central catalog of hospitality display models that can be pushed to the property

## 2. Warm leads

### Audience

Prospects who requested or completed a demo, still know LUCI, and are not in an active sales conversation.

### Strategy

For warm leads who have gone quiet, the campaign reopens the conversation with a clear account of what has changed. Anyone already engaged with sales remains in that conversation and does not enter an automated track.

### Story

LUCI has advanced since these prospects last saw it. LUCI already operates at Yaamava, Ameristar, Tachi Palace, and Sam's Town. The Field Activation Guide and The Signal reinforce that operating record.

### Assets and cadence

Two four-email tracks serve different points in the relationship:

- **Form track:** After a form submission receives no reply, allow approximately 10 quiet days, then begin four emails.
- **Post-demo track:** After a completed demo produces no next step, allow approximately 30 quiet days, then begin four emails.

A reply, scheduled meeting, or active deal ends either track. An email open alone does not trigger either track.

## 3. Demand gen

### Audience

Casino and property A/V and IT teams that are not already in a conversation with LUCI.

### Strategy

For people who have never seen LUCI, demand generation does not lead with New LUCI. It leads with the established LUCI offer and introduces the company through the website first, followed by outbound and then events.

### Story

**The Orchestration Engine for Enterprise Multimedia.**

**One interface to control, automate, and execute the entire guest experience.**

Platform and partnership as one offering.

### Assets and sequence

1. **Fall website:** Present the LUCI story, support search discovery, and provide a path to a demo.
2. **Outbound:** Begin after the website is in place.
3. **Events:** Follow outbound.

Public LinkedIn communication begins only when LUCI is ready to speak outside the customer base.
